# Editorial Acceptance

## Institution Intelligence Framework

**Acceptance date:** 18 July 2026

**Status:** Canonical

**Version:** 1.0

**Editorial lock:** Complete

## Accepted Scope

The accepted framework comprises:

- the Institution Intelligence Framework;
- the Public Source Policy;
- the Research Workflow;
- the Institution Intelligence Standard;
- the Strategic Gap Analysis Standard;
- the Editorial Mapping Standard;
- the Institution Edition Standard;
- the Commercial Proposal Standard;
- the Implementation Roadmap Standard; and
- the template repository structure and its standard scaffold.

The framework contains no institution-specific research, Institution Edition, proposal, commercial terms, implementation commitment, or production authorization.

## Editorial Determination

The Institution Intelligence Framework is accepted as the canonical research and editorial methodology for institution-specific editions within the x-change publishing program.

Future institution-specific work begins with evidence-based Institution Intelligence rather than manuscript writing.

## Publishing-System Relationship

The framework belongs within the larger publishing lineage:

```text
Movement
   ↓
Institutional Study
   ↓
Program Guide
   ↓
Institution Intelligence
   ↓
Institution Edition
   ↓
Commercial Proposal
   ↓
Implementation
```

Movements, Institutional Studies, and Program Guides establish or operationalize canonical doctrine. Institution Intelligence studies how a real institution relates to that doctrine. The Institution Edition adapts the approved Program Guide without altering its architecture or operating grammar.

## Evidence Rule

Institution Intelligence is based on evidence.

Every material institution-specific statement must distinguish among:

- **Observed** — directly supported by identified evidence;
- **Inferred** — a bounded interpretation of identified observations; and
- **Recommended** — a proposed action or application.

Inference must never be presented as fact. Institution-provided material remains separately identified and governed by its stated use conditions.

## Canonical Agent Rule

> If sufficient evidence is unavailable, the research agent shall explicitly identify the gap rather than compensate with unsupported prose.

Missing evidence, unresolved contradictions, incomplete lineage, and possible doctrinal conflicts must remain visible. A template must never be completed through speculation.

## Lock Consequences

- The accepted framework, standards, policies, workflow, and template structure are frozen.
- Future institution projects reuse the accepted standards rather than redesign them.
- Institution-specific evidence may evolve without changing the framework.
- Institution Editions, proposals, and implementation work remain downstream artifacts.
- Canonical doctrine remains governed by its source documents and Editorial Decision Records.
- A proposed framework change requires an explicit accepted Editorial Decision Record.

## Future Recommendation

After several institution-intelligence projects have been completed, editorial governance may consider creating—but does not presently authorize—`CANONICAL_INSTITUTION_INTELLIGENCE_TAXONOMY.md`.

Its possible purpose would be to standardize cross-institution comparison across categories such as ownership, business model, customer segments, products, channels, Treasury, payments, consumer banking, SME banking, housing, merchant services, technology, partnerships, financial indicators, strategic themes, and implementation signals.

This recommendation is deferred. No taxonomy document is created by this acceptance.
