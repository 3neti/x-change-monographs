# Edition Relationship — AllBank

## Purpose

This note defines the authority, scope, and lifecycle of the AllBank Institution Edition Research Edition. It prevents a research-based adaptation from being mistaken for canonical doctrine, an AllBank-approved publication, a commercial proposal, or an implementation commitment.

## Publication lineage

```text
Thrift Bank Institutional Study
            ↓
Canonical Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide
            ↓
AllBank Institution Intelligence
            ↓
AllBank Institution Edition — Research Edition
            ↓
Executive Discussion and Institution-Provided Evidence
            ↓
AllBank Institution Edition — Confirmed Edition
            ↓
Commercial Proposal
            ↓
Implementation
```

The Institutional Study remains the authoritative doctrinal source. The Program Guide operationalizes that doctrine. The Research Edition adapts the Program Guide to AllBank using classified public evidence. It does not supersede any canonical source.

## Research Edition

The Research Edition:

- records verified public observations about AllBank;
- presents bounded inferences without elevating them to fact;
- identifies possible areas for management consideration;
- makes uncertainty and evidence gaps visible;
- preserves the canonical authority, custody, settlement, operating-grammar, and readiness boundaries; and
- establishes an agenda for executive discussion.

It does not record AllBank approval, adoption, priority, architecture, integration feasibility, commercial terms, production scope, or readiness.

## Confirmed Edition

A future Confirmed Edition may incorporate institution-provided evidence after the provider, authority, date, approval state, permitted use, distribution restrictions, and supporting record have been documented. Confirmation may change institutional emphasis or examples. It may not silently alter canonical doctrine.

Material doctrinal change requires an Editorial Decision Record. Commercial commitments belong in a later proposal. Delivery commitments belong in an authorized implementation record.

## Canonical coverage parity

The Research Edition answers the canonical business questions at the level permitted by public evidence.

| Canonical Program Guide question | Research Edition treatment | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why examine the Program? | Executive Summary and Chapters 2–3 | Frames an inquiry; does not establish AllBank priority |
| What is the Program? | Chapter 3 | Preserves Program-versus-Capability and institutional boundaries |
| Why might the institution participate? | Chapters 3 and 5 | Presents potential institutional usefulness; no promised outcome |
| How might it work? | Chapters 4 and 6 | Uses canonical grammar; no AllBank architecture or driver claim |
| What would banking in action look like? | Chapter 4 | Compares candidate ceremonies; selects none |
| What is the institutional case? | Chapter 5 | Treats Transaction Gravity as a tendency; economics remain Unknown |
| How would consideration begin? | Chapter 7 and Conclusion | Authorizes dialogue and discovery only; no implementation sequence |

Commercial participation cost, quantified return, and implementation scope are not silently omitted. They are explicitly deferred because the public evidence does not establish AllBank economics, priorities, architecture, or an approved ceremony. Those matters belong after institutional confirmation and, where authorized, in a commercial proposal.

## Evidence rule

All institution-specific statements are classified as **Observed**, **Inferred**, **Recommended**, or **Unknown** and are traceable to the AllBank Evidence Register. If sufficient evidence is unavailable, the gap remains visible.
