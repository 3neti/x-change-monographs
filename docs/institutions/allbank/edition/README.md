# AllBank Institution Edition

This directory contains the first institution-specific adaptation of the canonical Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide.

## Current edition

- Edition: **Research Edition**
- Version: 0.9
- Status: **In Review**
- Basis: Prepared from Publicly Available Information
- Evidence cutoff: 18 July 2026

The Research Edition is an evidence-based discussion document for AllBank management. It records the institution as presently understood, distinguishes observation from inference and recommendation, and exposes matters that require institutional confirmation. It is not a proposal, a statement of AllBank intent, a Confirmed Edition, or an implementation plan.

## Editorial boundary

The manuscript may identify evidence-backed possibilities and questions. It may not state that AllBank has adopted the Program, selected a banking ceremony, approved an architecture, exposed suitable APIs, established an institution-specific driver, or reached operational readiness.

The next authorized activity is executive discussion and evidence confirmation. A Confirmed Edition may be prepared only after institution-provided evidence has been reviewed and classified.
