# Edition Relationship — AllBank Executive Edition

## Purpose

This note records the relationship between the canonical Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide, the AllBank Institution Intelligence repository, the internal AllBank Research Edition, and the client-facing AllBank Executive Edition.

## Editorial hierarchy

```text
Canonical Doctrine
        ↓
Canonical Thrift Bank Program Guide — Executive Edition
        ↓
AllBank Institution Intelligence
        ↓
AllBank Research Edition — Internal Working Publication
        ↓
AllBank Executive Edition — Client Publication
        ↓
Executive Discussion
        ↓
Future Confirmed or Commercial Work, if separately authorized
```

The canonical Program Guide remains the structural and doctrinal authority. Institution Intelligence and the Research Edition inform the adaptation; they do not become a second source of doctrine.

## Research Edition

The Research Edition has completed its working purpose. It preserves public evidence, observations, bounded inferences, recommendations, unknowns, and questions for management.

It remains an internal institutional-intelligence artifact. It is not distributed to AllBank unless specifically requested and approved for that purpose. It is not revised or superseded by the Executive Edition.

## Executive Edition

The Executive Edition is the client-facing Program Guide. It synthesizes institution-specific understanding into the calm, board-oriented voice of the canonical Executive Edition.

Its concise main body supports executive reading. Its embedded Annexes A–H preserve the canonical operating, ceremony, driver, governance, Treasury, readiness, and terminology references required for institutional evaluation.

It does not expose:

- evidence classifications;
- claim or source identifiers;
- evidence registers;
- internal recommendations;
- unknown registers;
- research methodology; or
- management-question inventories.

It may acknowledge natural institutional limits where public information cannot establish AllBank's internal priorities, architecture, APIs, settlement arrangements, economics, or readiness. Those limits are expressed as matters for institutional dialogue rather than as research apparatus.

## Doctrinal inheritance

The Executive Edition preserves without redefinition:

- the bank as protagonist;
- bank authority and settlement responsibility;
- x-change operating grammar;
- institution-specific driver responsibility;
- settlement connectivity as narrower than banking capability;
- the banking service as product;
- the Pay Code as credential;
- Treasury grammar;
- Transaction Gravity;
- Program-versus-Capability distinction; and
- operational readiness as an institutional judgment.

Institution-specific examples do not change these meanings.
