# Questions for Management — AllBank

## Purpose

These questions address matters not established by the reviewed public evidence. They are not assumptions, recommendations, implementation requirements, or a sales questionnaire. Answers should be classified as **Institution-Provided** and recorded with authority and use restrictions.

## Institutional direction

1. What are AllBank’s three most important institutional priorities for the next three years?
2. How does management rank deposit growth, consumer lending, housing, SME and corporate banking, payroll, merchant services, collections, remittance, and partner distribution?
3. Which customer and business relationships does management consider most strategically important?
4. Which products are being expanded, maintained, redesigned, limited, or retired?
5. What current legal and beneficial ownership disclosure should govern institution-specific publication?

Related gaps: UNK-001, UNK-005, UNK-011, UNK-016.

## Customers and channels

6. How many active retail, payroll, corporate, merchant, and mobile customers does AllBank serve?
7. What are the current branch, ATM, POS cash-out, merchant, and other physical-access counts?
8. What roles do branches, AllBank Mobile, e-KYC, AllConnect, the merchant portal, APIs, and partners play in each priority journey?
9. Where do customers leave a digital journey and require branch or operations assistance?
10. Which completion, abandonment, turnaround, complaint, dispute, and satisfaction measures are reviewed by management?
11. Which channels own customer communication and evidence when a transaction is delayed, rejected, duplicated, reversed, or uncertain?

Related gaps: UNK-002, UNK-004, UNK-009, UNK-010.

## Products and banking ceremonies

12. Which current service best combines institutional importance, bounded scope, operating familiarity, and readiness to learn?
13. For payroll, who funds the activity, who approves release, what cut-offs apply, and how are incomplete or rejected employee credits resolved?
14. For merchant payments, how are merchants onboarded, credited, notified, reconciled, supported, and charged?
15. For collections, how are payer identity, reference data, matching, exceptions, and client reporting governed?
16. For remittance, what service remains active, which partners and corridors apply, and how are sender funding and recipient eligibility governed?
17. For housing or SME disbursement, what documentary conditions, payees, release stages, and post-release evidence apply?
18. Does AllBank currently provide material government disbursement, government collection, or public-sector banking services?

Related gaps: UNK-005, UNK-009–UNK-011, UNK-013, UNK-016.

## Financial and relationship evidence

19. What are the current CASA mix, deposit concentration, loan-product mix, NPL measures, capital ratios, ROA, and revenue mix?
20. What measures show whether payroll, merchant, collection, remittance, housing, or business relationships lead to recurring deposits or additional service use?
21. What are the unit economics and principal operating costs of the candidate ceremonies?
22. Which service-level and relationship measures would management use to assess institutional usefulness rather than transaction volume alone?

Related gaps: UNK-003, UNK-004, UNK-009, UNK-012.

## Settlement and Treasury

23. Which settlement accounts, ACH arrangements, sponsor or service-provider relationships, and internal ledgers support the candidate services?
24. Who controls production credentials, limits, release authority, liquidity, and reconciliation?
25. How are settlement instructions attributed to the correct service, customer, merchant, employer, or partner?
26. How does Treasury recognize available resources, reservations, releases, returns, and uncertain outcomes?
27. Which external events would constitute Funding Events for the candidate ceremony?
28. How are failures, partial results, reversals, duplicates, time-outs, and delayed confirmation treated financially and operationally?

Related gaps: UNK-008, UNK-014.

## Technology and integration

29. What core-banking, payment, integration, channel, identity, fraud, and reconciliation systems support the candidate services?
30. Which systems and cloud environments are owned or controlled by AllBank, and which are provider-managed?
31. What approved APIs are available for account validation, instruction submission, status, balances, transaction history, reversals, and reconciliation?
32. What authentication, certificates, signatures, idempotency, webhooks, error models, limits, and versioning apply?
33. Who owns API change control, testing environments, certification, production release, and continuing maintenance?
34. Is any common orchestration or workflow layer already used across services?
35. Where does service-specific implementation currently duplicate identity, approval, evidence, exception, notification, or reconciliation work?
36. What observability, audit, operational support, and evidence-retention capabilities are available?

Related gaps: UNK-006, UNK-007.

## Risk, security, compliance, and resilience

37. Which independent security certifications, penetration tests, recovery tests, control assessments, and incident evidence are current?
38. Which risk, compliance, privacy, consumer-protection, AML, fraud, and information-security controls apply to each candidate ceremony?
39. Who decides whether a new service is operationally ready?
40. What evidence must Technology, Operations, Treasury, Risk, Compliance, Information Security, Internal Audit, and business owners review before controlled production?
41. What business-continuity, recovery, manual fallback, and customer-remediation procedures apply when a channel or provider is unavailable?

Related gaps: UNK-010, UNK-015.

## Governance and readiness

42. Which Board and management committees would authorize discovery, implementation, pilot, and production?
43. Who is accountable for the complete banking outcome, rather than only the channel or API?
44. What would constitute sufficient evidence to proceed, correct, extend, or pause a bounded pilot?
45. Which customer, branch, employer, merchant, value, time, and duration limits would be appropriate for controlled production?
46. Which matters require Board approval, management approval, regulator engagement, or third-party consent?

## Evidence handling

For every response, record:

- provider and authority;
- date provided;
- status as draft, approved, or other;
- whether it may be quoted, summarized, or used only for internal analysis;
- distribution restrictions;
- supporting documents or system evidence; and
- any conflict with the public source register.

If an answer remains unavailable, the related gap remains **Unknown**. It must not be replaced with inferred prose.
