## 1. Purpose

This Proposal is submitted by Open Disbursement Technologies, Inc. ("ODTI") to AllBank for the conduct of a controlled Proof of Concept under the Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program.

The Proof of Concept is intended to determine whether a reusable banking operating capability may support an agreed banking service within AllBank's institutional environment.

The Proof of Concept shall not constitute a production deployment, production license, or long-term commercial commitment.

The attached *Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide — AllBank Edition* provides the institutional and architectural context for this Proposal.

## 2. Offer

ODTI proposes to provide:

- a provisional license to the x-change platform;
- institutional discovery;
- joint selection of one banking ceremony;
- development and enablement of the institution-specific integration driver;
- installation and configuration of the platform;
- controlled execution of the Proof of Concept;
- technical support during the Proof of Concept; and
- technical and executive findings.

The banking ceremony shall be selected jointly by AllBank and ODTI. The Proof of Concept shall be limited to the scope agreed by the Parties.

## 3. Deliverables

Upon completion of the Proof of Concept, ODTI shall deliver:

- a configured Proof of Concept environment;
- an institution-specific integration driver for the agreed interface, where required;
- a demonstration of the agreed banking ceremony;
- technical findings;
- executive findings; and
- a recommendation for subsequent engagement.

The institution-specific integration driver shall be developed and enabled as part of the Proof of Concept. The driver shall remain a maintained component of the ODTI platform and shall be made available to AllBank under the applicable provisional or production license.

The initial driver enablement is included in the Proof of Concept. It shall not be described as free development.

Items outside the agreed Proof of Concept scope shall require separate written agreement.

## 7. Indicative Engagement Path

The Parties acknowledge the following possible progression:

```text
Stage 1                 Stage 2                 Stage 3
Proof of Concept   ->   Joint Evaluation   ->   Pilot Operations

Stage 4                 Stage 5                 Stage 6
Production License ->   Production Rollout ->   Expansion
```

### Stage 1 — Proof of Concept

The Parties establish technical feasibility and assess the institutional suitability of the selected banking ceremony.

### Stage 2 — Joint Evaluation

The Parties review the technical, operational, institutional, and commercial findings. The possible determinations are **Proceed**, **Correct**, **Pause**, or **Terminate**.

### Stage 3 — Pilot Operations

Subject to separate written approval, the Parties may conduct a limited production pilot under agreed operating conditions; customer or transaction limits; service scope; controls; support arrangements; success criteria; and review procedures.

### Stage 4 — Production License

If the Pilot produces satisfactory results, the Parties may negotiate and execute a definitive production license and applicable service agreements. The production agreement may address annual, account-based, site, or usage-based licensing; support; maintenance; driver maintenance; service levels; security; data treatment; commercial responsibilities; and other applicable terms.

### Stage 5 — Production Rollout

Following the required institutional approvals and execution of definitive agreements, AllBank may introduce the agreed banking service into controlled production according to its implementation priorities.

### Stage 6 — Expansion

AllBank may subsequently authorize additional banking ceremonies, customer groups, channels, branches, products, or use cases under separately agreed scope and commercial terms.

Successful completion of the Proof of Concept shall not obligate either Party to proceed to Pilot Operations, a Production License, Production Rollout, or Expansion. Each subsequent stage shall require separate written approval by both Parties.
