## Institutional Thesis

**Recommended — subject to review.** AllBank is an established multichannel thrift bank with visible household, employer, business, merchant, collection, remittance, and payment-network relationships. Its institution-specific opportunity is not to acquire banking authority or basic settlement connectivity. It is to determine whether one governed operating capability could help the bank organize additional banking responsibilities across existing channels and relationships while preserving AllBank’s authority, infrastructure, settlement arrangements, and customer ownership.

This thesis is supported by CLM-005–CLM-016 and INF-001–INF-004. It remains qualified by UNK-004–UNK-011.

## Protected Doctrine

An AllBank edition may not change or imply exceptions to these propositions:

- AllBank remains the protagonist.
- The bank already possesses banking authority.
- The bank brings or establishes its own settlement relationships and payment connectivity.
- x-change does not grant banking authority or settlement access.
- Settlement connectivity alone is not a banking service.
- The bank brings the authority and the rails; x-change brings the operating grammar; the bank-specific driver connects them.
- x-change is an operating capability beneath approved channels, not another customer channel.
- The banking service is the product; the Pay Code is the credential.
- Funds remain under bank custody.
- Facility, Settlement Envelope, Treasury, Funding Event, Lien, Pay Code, Execution Engine, and Execution Driver retain their canonical meanings.
- A bank-specific driver requires discovery, API mapping, implementation, certification, and continuing maintenance.
- Driver certification is not operational readiness.
- Readiness is an institutional judgment.
- One governed operating capability may support many banking services.
- Remittance remains the canonical explanatory ceremony, though it need not be the first production service.
- Transaction Gravity is a tendency, not a guaranteed commercial outcome.

## Permitted Adaptations

Subject to Editorial Mapping Review, the future edition may:

- use AllBank’s public language for corporate internet banking, QRPh, payroll, merchant, collection, and partner services;
- give greater weight to employer, merchant, business, and partner relationships;
- compare remittance, payroll, merchant payments, and collections as possible explanatory or starting ceremonies;
- recognize current InstaPay, PESONet, QR Ph, and Bills Pay PH participation without implying x-change supplies those rails;
- acknowledge AllBank’s mobile, e-KYC, corporate, merchant, and branch channels as existing institutional assets;
- adapt examples to a hybrid physical-digital service model; and
- change implementation emphasis only after institution-provided discovery establishes a reason.

It may not state that AllBank has selected a ceremony, approved x-change, exposed suitable APIs, established a driver, or reached production readiness.

## Audience Questions

The future edition must answer, using approved evidence:

1. What institutional responsibility would AllBank be considering?
2. How would the Program complement—not replace—AllBank’s current channels and payment participation?
3. Which recurring customer, employer, merchant, borrower, and business relationships matter most?
4. Why would one governed operating capability be preferable to separate service-specific solutions?
5. What would AllBank continue to own and control?
6. What institution-specific discovery and driver work would be required?
7. How would management distinguish technical integration from operational readiness?
8. Which bounded ceremony should demonstrate the model?
9. What evidence would the Board require before controlled production?
