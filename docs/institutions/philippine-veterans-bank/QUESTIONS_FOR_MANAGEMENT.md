# Philippine Veterans Bank — Questions for Management

## Purpose

These questions convert public-evidence limits into a controlled institution-discovery agenda. They do not presume PVB interest in x-change, authorize a proposal, or select a modernization scope.

Any response must be recorded as **Institution-Provided** unless independently supported as public **Observed** evidence. The provider, authority, date, confidentiality, permitted use, and downstream restrictions must be recorded.

## 1. Institutional direction and authority

1. Which strategic priorities have formal Board approval for 2026–2028?
2. How are military banking, public-sector banking, digital transformation, capital restoration, rebranding, and acquisition activity sequenced?
3. Which executive and Board committees sponsor government-depository services and digital modernization?
4. What authority would be required to begin an institution-specific capability assessment?
5. Which public-sector relationships does PVB consider strategic, and which are primarily transactional or historical?

## 2. Charter and government-depository operation

1. How does PVB operationalize RA 11597 and the current DOF government-depository rules at account and workflow level?
2. Which classes of public entities and public funds may PVB currently accept without further approvals, and which require additional authority?
3. What legal, Treasury, Finance, Compliance, and Audit reviews govern a new government deposit, collection, or disbursement arrangement?
4. How are public authority, bank authority, fund character, account ownership, and beneficiary entitlement kept distinct?
5. What government account types and Mandated Disbursement System arrangements are currently active?

## 3. Ownership, capital, and regulatory position

1. What is the current status of the Capital Build-Up Program and related BSP approvals?
2. Which portions of reported equity and qualifying capital are fully recognized for regulatory purposes?
3. What conditions remain attached to appraisal-increment recognition?
4. What regulatory requirements and sanctions disclosed at year-end 2025 remain outstanding?
5. What capital and operating budget is available for current transformation initiatives?
6. What is the current status of the UCPB Savings Bank acquisition, and what approvals remain?

## 4. Customers, constituencies, and economics

1. How many active customers fall within veteran, military, pensioner, retail, SME, corporate, LGU, NGA, GOCC, and cooperative segments?
2. What proportions of deposits, loans, fee income, and transaction activity arise from those segments?
3. What portion of deposits is public money, and how concentrated are those balances by entity and account type?
4. Which customer relationships are branch-led, employer-led, program-led, digital-led, or partner-led?
5. What are the retention, complaint, service, and cross-product measures for material constituencies?

## 5. Public-finance services

1. Which public disbursement, collection, payroll, pension, subsidy, scholarship, assistance, procurement, and supplier workflows are active?
2. For each workflow, what is the legal authority, account structure, funding source, approval chain, beneficiary model, and evidence requirement?
3. How are beneficiary data, eligibility, consent, privacy, corrections, and support governed?
4. How are unclaimed funds, failed payments, reversals, disputes, duplicate instructions, and unresolved outcomes handled?
5. What transaction volumes, values, service levels, fees, costs, and reconciliation timelines apply?
6. Which examples—Parañaque, Negros Occidental, Malabon, BARMM, CEPALCO, and others—remain active and at what scale?

## 6. Treasury, settlement, and reconciliation

1. Which accounts and legal entities hold funds for each service?
2. Who authorizes movement, and what limits, liquidity, prefunding, or reserve arrangements apply?
3. Which payment systems and providers are used directly or indirectly?
4. What status, exception, reversal, return, and dispute data is available from each provider?
5. How are bank records, provider records, public-entity records, beneficiary records, and the general ledger reconciled?
6. What evidence is retained for internal audit, external audit, regulators, and public authorities?

## 7. Products and operations

1. Which disclosed products are active, in pilot, restricted, partner-delivered, being replaced, or no longer offered?
2. What operational teams own account opening, payments, collections, disbursements, payroll, pensions, cards, kiosks, POS, and gateway services?
3. What are the operating hours, cutoffs, exception paths, escalation procedures, and support commitments?
4. How are branches and digital channels coordinated for assisted customers and public-program participants?
5. What is the reconciled branch and ATM footprint by ownership, operation, and service scope?

## 8. Digital banking and customer experience

1. What are current retail and institutional digital user counts, monthly active users, transaction counts, values, and completion rates?
2. What customer journeys are fully digital, assisted, or branch-dependent?
3. What are the principal failure, complaint, fraud, dispute, and abandonment points?
4. What features differ between retail and institutional platforms?
5. Which digital services have current regulatory approval, and what restrictions remain?
6. What is the roadmap for eSOA, instant card issuance, BDA, Bills Pay PH, InstaPay, gateway, kiosk, and other disclosed initiatives?

## 9. Technology architecture

1. What systems of record support customers, deposits, loans, cards, Treasury, GL, AML, fraud, channels, and document management?
2. Which systems are institution-owned, licensed, outsourced, hosted, or managed by providers?
3. What are the production hosting, network, cloud, data-center, and environment boundaries?
4. Who owns source code, configurations, data, credentials, secrets, certificates, and production approvals?
5. What APIs, files, queues, or other interfaces support inquiries, posting, payments, status, reversals, and reconciliation?
6. What sandbox, test data, certification, release, rollback, and change-management processes exist?
7. What logging, monitoring, tracing, alerting, and evidence-retention capabilities are available?

## 10. Security, risk, privacy, and continuity

1. What security governance and assurance covers digital and public-finance services?
2. How are identities, access, privileged roles, devices, credentials, and segregation of duties managed?
3. What fraud-system and AML-system coverage, tuning, escalation, and performance evidence exists?
4. What material incidents, near misses, audit findings, or remediation programs affect the proposed research domains?
5. What are the RTO, RPO, backup, disaster-recovery, and business-continuity requirements and test results?
6. What data privacy, retention, residency, sharing, and deletion rules apply to government and beneficiary data?

## 11. Providers and commercial relationships

1. What are the current roles of BancNet, PESONet, InstaPay participants, Paynamics, ECPay, and other providers?
2. Which relationships are exclusive, substitutable, or due for renewal?
3. What service levels, incident duties, liability, audit, data, security, and exit provisions apply?
4. How are provider fees and public-program economics allocated and governed?
5. Which providers would need to participate in any later capability discovery?

## 12. Institution-specific engagement boundary

1. Has PVB authorized any evaluation of x-change or the Government Depository Program?
2. If so, what institutional question is PVB seeking to answer?
3. What evidence may be shared, by whom, under what confidentiality and use conditions?
4. What decision would a discovery phase be expected to support?
5. What conditions would require the work to pause rather than proceed?

## Evidence-handling reminder

Answers should not be inserted directly into the intelligence report without provenance. Each material response must first enter an institution-provided source register and claim-level evidence record, with contradictions and permitted use stated.
