## Program Guide review

The PVB Executive Edition is frozen as the Version 1.0 First Edition rebuilt from the approved AllBank Executive Edition. The accepted Modernization Program remains its canonical PVB parent. Institution Intelligence, Strategic Assessment, Gap Analysis, and the Executive Submission Suite remain internal sources supporting accuracy and traceability; they do not appear in the external publication.

Direct editorial comparison, Executive Review Proof generation, institution-first cover review, and Publication Freeze are complete. The accepted Version 0.9 review proof remains preserved as publication history. Commercial and implementation work remains separate and may not alter the AllBank master, canonical Modernization Program, or frozen PVB Program Guide, or imply PVB adoption, procurement, production, or commercial authority.

No later-phase file should be created merely to demonstrate momentum.

## Evidence refresh triggers

Before any later phase begins, refresh the corpus if any of the following changes:

- audited 2026 financial statements become available;
- BSP acts on the disclosed capital build-up plan or regulatory applications;
- the UCPB Savings Bank acquisition is approved, completed, amended, or abandoned;
- PVB publishes a new ownership, Board, branch, or product disclosure;
- material public-finance partnerships move from announcement to evidenced operation;
- applicable government-depository rules change; or
- institution-provided discovery becomes authorized.
