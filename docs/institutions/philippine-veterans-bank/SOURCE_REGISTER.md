| Branch count | SRC-004, SRC-006, current website surfaces | SRC-004 describes 60 full branches plus one branch-lite; SRC-006 reports 61 branches; some current website text reports 60 branches. | Report as 61 locations when the full/branch-lite distinction matters: 60 full branches and one branch-lite as of 31 Dec 2025. | ATM count | SRC-006, current website surfaces | SRC-006 reports 281 ATMs (68 onsite, 213 offsite) as of 31 Dec 2025; some website text reports 201 ATMs. | Use the dated SEC disclosure and retain the website discrepancy. | Capitalization | SRC-004, SRC-006, SRC-013 | SRC-013 announced achievement of a ₱10 billion capitalization target, while audited disclosures distinguish equity, qualifying capital, minimum capitalization, deposits for stock subscription, appraisal increment, and pending BSP approvals. | Use the audited components and regulatory qualifications. Do not repeat the press-release phrase as equivalent to audited equity or qualifying capital. | Capital definitions and regulatory treatment must remain separate. |
| Loan measures | SRC-004, SRC-006, SRC-009 | Audited consolidated net loans, information-statement published statement-of-condition loans, and BSP bank-only net loans differ by perimeter and definition. | Present each with its source, period, perimeter, and definition. | Digital readiness | SRC-004, SRC-006, SRC-011 | Management described digital infrastructure as complete and ready for enhanced products; audited operational sections also identify regulatory applications and continuing initiatives. | Treat completion language as management's stated direction, not evidence that every license, integration, control, or production-readiness condition is complete. | Preserve separate facts for launched services, pending applications, and unknown architecture. |

## Source limitations

The source set is strong for legal identity, disclosed strategy, historical and reporting-period financials, visible products, and selected partnerships. It remains insufficient for production architecture, system ownership, API and provider contracts, public-fund account structures, transaction-level performance, procurement posture, or institution interest in x-change. Those matters remain Unknown pending authorized institution discovery.
