## Constituency ecosystem

- **Observed.** The charter and disclosures connect PVB with World War II veterans, post-World War II veterans, AFP retirees, active military personnel, pensioners, compulsory heirs, and recognized veterans organizations. [CLM-002, CLM-005, CLM-011, CLM-023]
- **Observed.** Products and outreach extend to LGUs, NGAs, GOCCs, cooperatives, private corporations, small businesses, public-school personnel, and other public constituencies. [CLM-011–CLM-013]
- **Inferred.** PVB's relationship network spans identity-based, employer-based, pension-based, public-authority, provider, and commercial relationships. This breadth is a strategic asset and a governance burden. [CLM-043–CLM-044]

## Observed implications

- **Inferred.** PVB has practical experience coordinating bank services with public institutions and external payment providers. Public evidence does not reveal whether those arrangements use a common operating model or remain bespoke. [CLM-019–CLM-022, CLM-046]
- **Inferred.** The military and veterans ecosystem may give PVB a distinct basis for public-service relationships that larger generalist banks do not replicate in the same form. The size and commercial effect of that advantage are Unknown. [CLM-023, CLM-047]
- **Inferred.** The proposed UCPB Savings acquisition could materially affect branches, customers, systems, public-school lending, and agricultural relationships. Its outcome must be refreshed before later analysis. [CLM-025]
