# Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Workstream — Compass

## Purpose

This Compass preserves the direction and boundaries of the Philippine Veterans Bank evidence, assessment, and modernization workstream.

## Governing question

Phase 1 asked:

> What does attributable public evidence establish about Philippine Veterans Bank as an institution, a private commercial bank, and an official government depository as of 20 July 2026?

Phase 2 asks:

> What strategic position, strengths, constraints, public-finance relevance, maturity signals, Unknowns, and tensions emerge from the accepted evidence when PVB is assessed on its own terms?

Comparison with the canonical Government Depository Program is reserved for Gap Analysis and is not performed at Gate 1.

Phase 3 asks:

> Where does the evidenced institution align with the canonical Government Depository capability, where is capability only partially evidenced, and where must Unknowns or proposed capabilities remain explicit?

Phase 4 asks:

> What controlled institutional response follows from the evidence, assessment, and comparison without presuming PVB approval, implementation, or readiness?

Phase 5 asks:

> What should PVB's Board or Management decide after reading the accepted Modernization Program?

## Protagonist

The protagonist is Philippine Veterans Bank as an institution. x-change is not the subject of Phase 1 or Phase 2.

## Evidence rule

Evidence precedes interpretation. Interpretation remains bounded. Unknowns remain visible.

Authority to receive government funds does not establish selection for a particular public purpose. A disclosed product does not establish customer adoption, profitability, operating readiness, or architecture. A stated strategy does not establish completion. A public partnership does not establish its continuing scope or performance beyond the disclosed evidence.

## Phase boundary

Completed in Phase 1:

- source discovery and provenance;
- claim-level evidence extraction;
- institutional description;
- financial and regulatory disclosure summary;
- product, channel, public-finance, governance, and technology signal inventory;
- bounded inference;
- contradictions and Unknowns;
- management questions; and
- Phase 1 gate assessment.

Authorized in Phase 2:

- evidence-led institutional interpretation;
- strategic strengths and constraints;
- public-finance relevance;
- financial, regulatory, digital, technology, and sovereignty assessment;
- strategic tensions;
- candidate domains preserved for later comparison;
- material Unknowns and management-validation requirements; and
- a Strategic Assessment Decision Record.

Completed under Gate 2 authority:

- comparison of PVB evidence with canonical Government Depository capability;
- classification as Aligned, Partially Evidenced, Unknown, Management Validation Required, Outside Current Authority, or Proposed Program Capability;
- assessment of governance, public-finance participation, Treasury grammar, execution, sovereignty, deployment, readiness, and ceremony relevance;
- preservation of management-validation questions; and
- a Gap Analysis Decision Record.

Completed under Gate 3 authority:

- explicit synthesis of Institution Intelligence, Strategic Assessment, and Gap Analysis;
- an institution-specific proposed Capability derived from canonical doctrine;
- evidence-supported candidate-ceremony recommendations;
- PVB-specific governance, sovereignty, readiness, and controlled-implementation principles;
- Management Validation and Unknowns preserved as decision conditions;
- a Modernization Program Decision Record; and
- a post-draft AllBank review limited to publication quality.

Completed under Phase 5 authority:

- a derivative Executive Summary;
- a formal Board Paper;
- an Executive Brief and One-Page Decision Memo;
- a Meeting Leave-Behind;
- an Executive Presentation Outline;
- an Executive FAQ; and
- suite-specific metadata, Compass, Roadmap, and audience governance.

Not authorized:

- institution-specific doctrine;
- competing PVB Program or institution-specific strategy;
- implementation presentation or proof of concept;
- commercial proposal;
- solution architecture; or
- implementation roadmap.

Gate 1 accepted the Strategic Assessment for comparison. Gate 2 accepted the Gap Analysis for institutional design. Gate 3 accepted the Modernization Program as canonical and authorized derivative executive communication. The Phase 5 suite is complete for review; publication production, commercial work, and implementation remain unauthorized.

## Protected canonical source

The Government Depository Program Guide and its supporting doctrine remain unchanged. This dossier may cite that lineage as a research lens, but it may not use the Guide to manufacture PVB facts.

## Phase 5 completion standard

The Executive Submission Suite is complete for review when:

1. every derivative names its audience and canonical parent;
2. every executive recommendation remains traceable to the Program;
3. the bounded Management-validation decision remains consistent;
4. no new evidence, conclusion, architecture, ceremony priority, or implementation step appears;
5. Unknowns and readiness boundaries remain visible;
6. technical detail does not displace the institutional decision;
7. no commercial term or production authority is implied; and
8. the Program remains read-only and authoritative.

Publication freeze and production require separate acceptance of the suite.
