# Philippine Veterans Bank Strategic Assessment Decision Record

## Purpose

This record preserves the material methodological and interpretive decisions used to prepare the Philippine Veterans Bank Strategic Assessment. It records how the Phase 1 evidence was interpreted. It does not record Program decisions, recommendations, or implementation choices.

## Status model

- **Accepted for Assessment** — applied in the Gate 1 Strategic Assessment;
- **Open** — requires a later editorial or management decision; and
- **Superseded** — replaced by a later recorded decision.

## Decision register

### SAD-001 — Preserve the phase sequence

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Strategic Assessment remains separate from Gap Analysis and Program design.

**Rationale:** The assessment must first interpret PVB on its own terms. Comparing it with a proposed capability before that interpretation is reviewed would blur evidence, diagnosis, and recommendation.

**Consequence:** No capability comparison, proposed x-change solution, Program structure, or executive submission is included.

### SAD-002 — Treat PVB's identity as one institutional whole

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Assess PVB as a private commercial bank, statutory veterans institution, and official government depository without allowing any one identity to replace the others.

**Rationale:** The three attributes are all established by the evidence and jointly explain PVB's institutional position.

**Consequence:** The assessment does not flatten PVB into a generic commercial bank or assume that its statutory identity establishes operating readiness.

### SAD-003 — Separate legal authority from selection and readiness

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Treat official government-depository status as material institutional authority, not as evidence of selection, entitlement, workflow readiness, or operating performance.

**Rationale:** The canonical Government Depository doctrine and the PVB evidence both require these distinctions.

**Consequence:** Public-finance relevance is assessed from the combination of authority and evidenced activity. Readiness remains unproven.

### SAD-004 — Balance recovery evidence with disclosed constraints

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Preserve improved profitability, balance-sheet scale, and liquidity alongside the qualified audit opinion, material uncertainty related to going concern, leverage shortfall, capital dependencies, and asset-quality context.

**Rationale:** Selecting only positive or only adverse indicators would distort the institution's disclosed position.

**Consequence:** The assessment recognizes operating recovery without converting it into a conclusion about unrestricted readiness.

### SAD-005 — Assess digital maturity by dimension

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Do not assign PVB one generalized digital-maturity label.

**Rationale:** Public evidence establishes visible channels, payment reach, public-finance applications, and active modernization initiatives, but does not establish operating performance, production architecture, or control maturity.

**Consequence:** Channel, payment, public-finance, regulatory, operating, and architectural maturity are assessed separately.

### SAD-006 — Treat public-finance evidence as selective but substantive

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Recognize that PVB's public-finance relevance extends beyond charter authority while avoiding a claim of comprehensive public-finance capability.

**Rationale:** Specific lending, assistance, collection, gateway, kiosk, ATM, pension, payroll, military, and public-institution relationships are evidenced. Their scale, continuity, economics, and performance are not fully established.

**Consequence:** The assessment describes demonstrated participation and preserves operating depth as a matter for validation.

### SAD-007 — Preserve operating sovereignty as Unknown

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Do not infer operating sovereignty from PVB's banking status, visible digital channels, or use of named providers.

**Rationale:** Sovereignty depends on actual custody, authority, infrastructure, credential, deployment, data, evidence, recovery, and provider-control boundaries, none of which are fully established by public evidence.

**Consequence:** Operating sovereignty is a management-validation domain rather than a finding of alignment or deficiency.

### SAD-008 — Do not select a banking ceremony

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Preserve veterans benefits, government payroll, LGU and public assistance, government collections, treasury and GOCC services, and procurement settlement as candidate domains only.

**Rationale:** Evidence supports varying degrees of institutional relevance but does not establish authority, priority, bounded scope, operational familiarity, or readiness for a first ceremony.

**Consequence:** Selection remains subject to later comparison and management validation.

### SAD-009 — Use strategic tensions instead of premature recommendations

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Express unresolved institutional relationships as strategic tensions.

**Rationale:** Tensions show what the institution must hold together without pretending that public evidence determines the appropriate solution.

**Consequence:** The assessment identifies matters for Board and management consideration but does not prescribe a Program.

### SAD-010 — Keep the AllBank benchmark closed

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Do not consult the AllBank Program during Strategic Assessment.

**Rationale:** The governing assignment permits its use only after the first complete PVB Modernization Program draft and only as an editorial benchmark.

**Consequence:** No AllBank-specific content, assumptions, structure, or strategic reasoning entered this assessment.

### SAD-011 — Preserve Phase 1 review status

**Status:** Accepted for Assessment

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Record the new assignment as limited authority to prepare Phase 2 without retroactively marking the Phase 1 dossier Accepted for Analysis.

**Rationale:** The assignment states that Phase 1 remains under review while explicitly authorizing the Strategic Assessment workstream.

**Consequence:** Phase 1 remains `In Review`; the Strategic Assessment is complete for Gate 1 review; Gap Analysis remains unauthorized.

## Open assessment decisions

### SAD-O01 — Gate 1 disposition

**Status:** Accepted for Gap Analysis

Gate 1 was reviewed and approved on 20 July 2026. The approval authorized evidence-led Gap Analysis with Unknowns preserved. It did not authorize Program drafting.

### SAD-O02 — Timing of management validation

**Status:** Open

The reviewer must determine which material Unknowns may remain open during Gap Analysis and which require institution-provided evidence before comparison begins.

### SAD-O03 — Candidate-ceremony evaluation

**Status:** Open

No ceremony may be selected until the authorized comparison evaluates institutional relevance, authority, relationship evidence, bounded scope, operating familiarity, management validation, and readiness.
