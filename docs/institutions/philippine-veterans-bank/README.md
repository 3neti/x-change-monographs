# Philippine Veterans Bank Institution Intelligence

This repository is the first evidence-based application of the canonical [Institution Intelligence Framework](../FRAMEWORK.md) to a government depository institution.

**Institution:** Philippine Veterans Bank (PVB)

**Phase:** 1 — Institution Intelligence

**Status:** Complete for review; not yet accepted for analysis

**Evidence cutoff:** 20 July 2026

**Public-source basis:** Yes; no institution-provided materials were used

The work describes PVB before evaluating it. It records what authoritative public evidence establishes, what may be inferred within that evidence, and what remains unknown. It does not contain a Strategic Assessment, Gap Analysis, Modernization Program, Institution Edition, proposal, or implementation plan.

## Evidence discipline

Every material institution-specific statement is classified as:

- **Observed** — directly supported by an identified public source;
- **Inferred** — a bounded interpretation of identified observations;
- **Recommended** — reserved for later authorized work and not used to advance a solution in this Phase 1 corpus;
- **Unknown** — not established by available public evidence;
- **Institution-Provided** — none in this phase; or
- **Calculated** — derived transparently from cited inputs.

The source and evidence registers are authoritative for claim provenance. Supporting notes provide readable domain views; they do not create a separate source of truth.

## Material limits

Public evidence establishes PVB's legal identity, government-depository authority, disclosed business lines, reporting-period financial position, visible digital services, and selected public-sector relationships. It does not establish complete production architecture, API specifications, settlement-account structures, system ownership, service-level performance, customer adoption by segment, economics of public-finance workflows, procurement posture, or willingness to adopt the canonical Program.

The 2025 audited financial statements contain a qualified opinion and a material uncertainty related to going concern. Those disclosures are preserved in the dossier with their stated context. They are not converted into a credit opinion or legal conclusion.

## Gate status

The Phase 1 package is complete for editorial and evidence review. Progression to Strategic Assessment requires an explicit **Accepted for Analysis** decision under the canonical framework. Until that decision is recorded, later phases remain unauthorized.
