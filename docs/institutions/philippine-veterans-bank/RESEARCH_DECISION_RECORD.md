## Purpose

This record explains the methodological decisions that shaped the Philippine Veterans Bank Institution Intelligence dossier.

It records **how the research was governed**. It does not add institutional facts, reinterpret evidence, revise conclusions, perform Strategic Assessment, or recommend an institutional action.

A future researcher should be able to determine why a source was preferred, why a conflict remained visible, why two figures were not combined, or why a question remained Unknown without reconstructing the reasoning from commit history.

## Research principles applied

1. **Evidence precedes interpretation.** Institution description begins with attributable evidence rather than a desired institutional narrative.
2. **Stronger sources govern narrower claims.** Applicable law, official regulatory records, and audited disclosures take precedence when they address the same matter as a less authoritative source.
3. **Source authority is claim-specific.** An audited annual report may provide high-confidence historical financial evidence while providing no evidence of an undisclosed production architecture.
4. **Uncertainty remains visible.** A contradiction or material absence becomes a recorded limitation or Unknown rather than an editorial gap to be completed through assumption.
5. **Evidence class and confidence remain distinct.** Observed, Inferred, Recommended, Unknown, Institution-Provided, and Calculated describe the nature of a statement; confidence describes the strength of support for its stated period and scope.
6. **Reporting periods, perimeters, and definitions remain intact.** Figures are not merged merely because their labels appear similar.
7. **Legal authority does not establish operational readiness.** Charter authority, regulatory eligibility, product availability, technical connectivity, selection, and production readiness are separate propositions.
8. **Visible channels do not reveal internal architecture.** Public product and technology signals support bounded observations only.
9. **Canonical doctrine remains separate from institution evidence.** The Government Depository Program Guide supplies a later analytical lens; it does not create facts about PVB.
10. **Each phase requires its own authority.** Completion of Institution Intelligence does not automatically authorize Strategic Assessment, Gap Analysis, an Institution Edition, a proposal, or implementation.

## Decision register

### RD-001 — Preserve the canonical phase boundary

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Keep Institution Intelligence separate from Strategic Assessment and every later artifact.

**Rationale:** Phase 1 is intended to describe the institution before evaluating its strategic position or fit with a canonical Program. Combining the phases would allow analytical or commercial assumptions to influence the evidence record.

**Alternatives considered:** Include preliminary strengths, gaps, x-change applications, or a modernization thesis in the Phase 1 report.

**Consequence:** The dossier records evidence-based attributes, bounded implications, Unknowns, and questions for management. No Strategic Assessment, Gap Analysis, Modernization Program, Institution Edition, proposal, or implementation plan was created.

### RD-002 — Treat the Government Depository Program Guide as a lens, not evidence

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Use the Government Depository Program Guide to identify research domains and institutional boundaries, but never cite it as proof of PVB intent, capability, adoption, selection, or readiness.

**Rationale:** Canonical doctrine explains the approved institutional model. It cannot establish institution-specific facts.

**Alternatives considered:** Treat references to PVB in Movement IV or the Program Guide as evidence of institutional alignment.

**Consequence:** Every PVB-specific claim remains traceable to law, regulatory material, audited disclosure, an institution publication, or another registered public source.

### RD-003 — Use the canonical institution repository hierarchy

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Place the dossier under `docs/institutions/philippine-veterans-bank/` rather than the assignment's suggested `docs/editorial/institution-intelligence/` path.

**Rationale:** The locked Institution Intelligence Framework designates `docs/institutions/` and its mandatory template as the source hierarchy for institution applications. The assignment allowed a different path where an established repository convention existed.

**Alternatives considered:** Create a parallel institution-intelligence hierarchy under `docs/editorial/`.

**Consequence:** The PVB dossier remains comparable with the AllBank application and does not fork the canonical publishing architecture.

### RD-004 — Prefer claim-specific primary authority

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Give controlling weight to the Revised Charter for legal identity, official regulatory records for regulatory statistics and issuances, audited statements for reporting-period financials, and dated SEC disclosures for ownership and governance.

**Rationale:** A single source is not strongest for every question. Claim-specific authority provides more reliable provenance than treating an institution website or secondary article as universally controlling.

**Alternatives considered:** Use the PVB website as the primary source for all current facts or rely on one annual report as the complete institutional record.

**Consequence:** The Source Register records hierarchy, claim scope, dates, and limitations for each source. Weaker sources identify or corroborate claims without displacing stronger evidence.

### RD-005 — Preserve the 20 July 2026 evidence cutoff

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Treat 20 July 2026 as the Phase 1 evidence cutoff and record earlier reporting dates separately.

**Rationale:** A fixed cutoff makes the dossier reproducible and prevents later information from being silently folded into a historical research baseline.

**Alternatives considered:** Describe every source as current without distinguishing reporting period, publication date, and access date.

**Consequence:** Future information must enter through an explicit refresh or new version rather than retroactively changing the meaning of the Phase 1 record.

### RD-006 — Do not merge incompatible financial perimeters

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Present audited consolidated figures at 31 December 2025 separately from BSP bank-group rankings at 31 March 2026 and separately from other published statements of condition.

**Rationale:** The figures differ by reporting date, consolidation perimeter, regulatory presentation, and sometimes definition. A continuous series would imply comparability that the public record does not establish.

**Alternatives considered:** Calculate growth from the 2025 annual report directly to the March 2026 BSP statistics or select whichever figure appeared most recent.

**Consequence:** Each table preserves period, unit, perimeter, definition, and limitation. No cross-perimeter growth calculation appears in the dossier.

### RD-007 — Preserve conflicting footprint and capital evidence

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Record branch, ATM, and capitalization conflicts rather than forcing a single unqualified statement.

**Rationale:** Dated disclosures report 60 full branches plus one branch-lite and 281 ATMs, while some website surfaces report lower counts. A public announcement that a ₱10 billion capitalization target was achieved is not equivalent to audited equity, qualifying capital, authorized capital, or final regulatory treatment.

**Alternatives considered:** Use the highest, lowest, newest-looking, or most favorable number without qualification.

**Consequence:** The strongest dated source governs the bounded historical statement, while the conflicting evidence and need for management confirmation remain visible.

### RD-008 — Present audit qualifications beside positive financial indicators

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Record improved earnings, assets, deposits, equity, and liquidity alongside the qualified audit opinion, material uncertainty related to going concern, capital dependencies, regulatory non-compliance, and sanctions disclosed for the same period.

**Rationale:** Omitting either side would distort the reporting-period evidence. Positive performance does not erase audit and prudential qualifications; the qualifications do not make the reported operational progress irrelevant.

**Alternatives considered:** Lead only with recovery indicators or characterize the institution primarily through the audit qualifications.

**Consequence:** The dossier provides a balanced institutional record without converting the evidence into a credit opinion or prudential conclusion.

### RD-009 — Do not calculate an NPL ratio from uncertain denominators

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Reproduce the disclosed gross NPL amount but do not calculate a dossier NPL ratio.

**Rationale:** Public materials contain multiple loan measures and definitions. Selecting a denominator without a controlled reconciliation could produce a precise but misleading result.

**Alternatives considered:** Divide the disclosed NPL amount by gross loans, net loans, or another published portfolio measure.

**Consequence:** Asset-quality evidence remains factual and period-specific. A ratio requires an authoritative disclosed measure or institution-confirmed reconciliation.

### RD-010 — Bound public technology evidence

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Treat digital channels, named systems, payment participation, security terms, and provider announcements as technology signals rather than proof of production architecture or readiness.

**Rationale:** A visible capability does not establish system ownership, hosting, APIs, credentials, data boundaries, DevOps, resilience, control effectiveness, settlement structure, or institution-specific driver feasibility.

**Alternatives considered:** Infer an internal architecture from named vendors, payment rails, product terms, or launch announcements.

**Consequence:** Internal architecture and implementation questions are classified Unknown and transferred to the management-discovery agenda.

### RD-011 — Separate authority, eligibility, selection, and readiness

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Treat PVB's private commercial-bank status, official government-depository authority, eligibility for particular public relationships, actual selection, current operating capability, and production readiness as distinct propositions.

**Rationale:** RA 11597 and DOF rules establish authority and a legal framework. They do not require a public institution to choose PVB or demonstrate that every workflow is operationally ready.

**Alternatives considered:** Describe government-depository status as evidence that PVB is automatically qualified, selected, or ready for any public-finance service.

**Consequence:** Public-sector examples are recorded as bounded evidence of activity, while account-level authority and readiness remain subject to applicable decisions and discovery.

### RD-012 — Bound partnership and program announcements

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Treat the Parañaque, Negros Occidental, Malabon, BARMM, AFP, CEPALCO, Paynamics, and ECPay references according to the exact scope and date disclosed.

**Rationale:** An announcement or annual-report description can establish a named relationship, pilot, launch, or provider role. It cannot establish current scale, exclusivity, economics, service quality, contract terms, or continuing status unless those matters are also disclosed.

**Alternatives considered:** Generalize named examples into a bank-wide maturity claim.

**Consequence:** Each relationship record states what the evidence supports and what remains Unknown.

### RD-013 — Keep recommendations out of Phase 1

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Use the required “Opportunities for Canonical Alignment” section only to identify later research domains, not to recommend x-change, a ceremony, vendor, architecture, or implementation sequence.

**Rationale:** The assignment expressly required research before recommendation. The Institution Intelligence Standard requires the section, but it does not require premature solution selection.

**Alternatives considered:** Populate the section with prospective x-change use cases or modernization priorities.

**Consequence:** The report states that no recommendation is authorized and treats possible alignment only as a future analytical question.

### RD-014 — Retain an In Review gate status

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Mark the Phase 1 package **Complete — In Review**, not **Accepted for Analysis**.

**Rationale:** Artifact completion and gate acceptance are separate governance events. The research team cannot self-authorize the next phase merely by completing the dossier.

**Alternatives considered:** Set the status to Accepted for Analysis because all required sections were populated.

**Consequence:** Strategic Assessment remains unauthorized until an explicit reviewer records the gate decision.

### RD-015 — Keep external evidence copies outside the canonical repository

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Commit source provenance, page references, claim mappings, and limitations, but not temporary local copies of third-party source documents.

**Rationale:** The repository's canonical evidence governance is the Source and Evidence Registers. External documents may change, carry separate rights, or already have stable official locations.

**Alternatives considered:** Commit downloaded annual reports, filings, and regulatory PDFs into the institution directory.

**Consequence:** The dossier remains reproducible through registered URLs and page references without turning third-party files into repository-owned canonical sources. Lawful retained copies may be governed separately if later required.
