# Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program Decision Record

## Purpose

This record preserves the major methodological and institutional-design decisions used to derive the Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program from accepted evidence, assessment, comparison, and canonical doctrine.

It is not a meeting record, commercial proposal, implementation plan, or production authorization.

## Status model

- **Accepted for Program Draft** — applied in the Gate 3 draft;
- **Open** — requires editorial or PVB management decision; and
- **Superseded** — replaced by a later recorded decision.

## Decision register

### MPD-001 — Require the Program to begin with evidence synthesis

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Introduce the proposed Capability only after summarizing what was observed, what the evidence means, and how PVB compares with the canonical framework.

**Rationale:** The Program must visibly emerge from the Institution Intelligence, Strategic Assessment, and Gap Analysis rather than appear predetermined.

**Consequence:** The Executive Summary and Section 1 preserve the full evidentiary sequence before introducing the institutional response.

### MPD-002 — Preserve PVB's three-part identity

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Design the Program for PVB as a private commercial bank, statutory veterans institution, and official government depository.

**Rationale:** The accepted evidence shows that these identities operate together and distinguish PVB's institutional position.

**Consequence:** The Program does not reduce PVB to a generic commercial bank or a government instrumentality.

### MPD-003 — Propose capability evaluation before implementation

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Make the immediate institutional response an evaluation of one reusable governed Capability rather than an implementation commitment.

**Rationale:** The Gap Analysis establishes institutional relevance while preserving material Unknowns about authority, architecture, sovereignty, economics, operating performance, and readiness.

**Consequence:** The decisions requested concern Management validation and a separate return decision.

### MPD-004 — Select bounded public assistance as the primary discovery candidate

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Recommend one bounded public-assistance disbursement ceremony as the primary candidate for institutional discovery, not production.

**Rationale:** PVB has direct evidence of a Parañaque student-allowance pilot and a BARMM financial-assistance relationship. The domain is publicly important, potentially bounded, and capable of exposing the complete operating discipline. [CLM-019, CLM-021]

**Alternatives considered:** Veterans' benefits, government payroll, public collections, Treasury and GOCC activity, and procurement settlement.

**Consequence:** Suitability remains subject to confirmation of current relationship, authority, funds, participants, accounts, evidence, exceptions, reconciliation, and readiness.

### MPD-005 — Preserve public collections as the secondary candidate

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Retain public collections as a secondary discovery candidate.

**Rationale:** Bills Pay PH, a city-hall kiosk, a provincial gateway arrangement, and named providers give the domain multiple PVB-specific evidence points. [CLM-017, CLM-018, CLM-020, CLM-022]

**Consequence:** Collections are not selected as the first ceremony and require current operating and economic validation.

### MPD-006 — Preserve veterans benefits and payroll as strategic later candidates

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Recognize veterans' pensions or benefits and government or military payroll as high-identity candidates without prioritizing them ahead of stronger workflow evidence.

**Rationale:** Their relevance to PVB is strong, but current program authority, workflow, scale, operations, and readiness are not sufficiently evidenced. [CLM-005, CLM-011, CLM-012, CLM-023]

**Consequence:** Identity does not substitute for ceremony evidence.

### MPD-007 — Defer procurement settlement

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Do not include procurement settlement in the initial discovery sequence.

**Rationale:** It is present in canonical doctrine, but the PVB evidence corpus does not establish a current procurement-settlement workflow.

**Consequence:** The domain remains available for later assessment if PVB-specific evidence is supplied.

### MPD-008 — Preserve canonical authority and custody boundaries

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Keep public authority with the responsible government entity and banking authority, custody, accounts, settlement relationships, production control, and readiness with PVB.

**Rationale:** The boundaries are canonical and consistent with PVB's institutional status.

**Consequence:** Neither x-change nor any provider is presented as government entity, bank, custodian, settlement authority, or production decision-maker.

### MPD-009 — Treat operating sovereignty as a Program requirement

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Require institution-controlled operating boundaries in the proposed model while preserving PVB's current sovereignty position as Unknown.

**Rationale:** The canonical framework defines the required boundary, while public evidence does not establish PVB's present environment, data, credential, deployment, provider, recovery, and audit controls. [CLM-046]

**Consequence:** Proposed control is not misrepresented as current fact.

### MPD-010 — Use readiness gates without creating an implementation plan

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Describe the institutional progression from validation to bounded production without dates, budgets, technical tasks, or commitments.

**Rationale:** The Board must understand decision boundaries, while implementation planning remains unauthorized and materially dependent on Unknowns.

**Consequence:** Controlled Implementation describes governance philosophy rather than a project roadmap.

### MPD-011 — Keep economic value contingent

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Present capability, accountability, and adaptability as the institutional case before discussing possible economic effects.

**Rationale:** PVB-specific workflow costs, deposits, fees, provider charges, volumes, and returns are not established. [CLM-047, CLM-049]

**Consequence:** The Program makes no promise of deposits, preferred status, revenue, or return.

### MPD-012 — Delay the AllBank quality review until the PVB draft is complete

**Status:** Accepted for Program Draft

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Complete the PVB-specific manuscript and decision record before consulting the AllBank Program.

**Rationale:** AllBank is authorized only as a publication-quality benchmark and must not shape PVB evidence, recommendations, ceremonies, priorities, or conclusions.

**Consequence:** The comparison was performed only after the first complete PVB draft. It was limited to rhythm, cadence, completeness, transitions, and appendix organization and is recorded in editorial provenance. No AllBank-specific institutional content entered the Program.

**Status:** Open

**Status:** Open
