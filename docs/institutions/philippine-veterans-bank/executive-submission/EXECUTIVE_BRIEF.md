# Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program

## Executive Brief

**Audience:** President, CEO, Executive Committee, and Strategy Office

**Purpose:** Prepare Management for a focused institutional discussion

**Canonical parent:** [Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program](../PHILIPPINE_VETERANS_BANK_MODERNIZATION_PROGRAM.md)

## The institutional question

PVB already possesses banking authority, official government-depository status, a continuing veterans purpose, public-sector relationships, and visible digital and payment capabilities.

The question is whether selected public-finance services should be governed through one reusable operating Capability rather than supported by separate operating foundations for each workflow.

## Why the question belongs to PVB

PVB's relevance comes from the combination of:

- private commercial-bank authority;
- statutory responsibility to the veterans community;
- official government-depository status;
- military, pensioner, government, LGU, and institutional relationships; and
- evidenced assistance, collection, gateway, kiosk, payroll, pension, financing, and digital activity.

This position creates a credible basis for evaluation. It does not establish government selection, implementation readiness, or entitlement to public-finance activity.

## The accepted Program

The Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program proposes one maintained operating foundation relating:

```text
Public Authority
        ↓
PVB Banking Service
        ↓
Governed Operating Capability
        ↓
PVB-Specific Execution Capability
        ↓
PVB-Approved Settlement Arrangement
```

Government entities retain public purpose, participant basis, approvals, conditions, and public accountability. PVB retains banking authority, custody, accounts, infrastructure, data, credentials, settlement relationships, production decisions, records, and readiness.

## Where validation should begin

The Program identifies one bounded public-assistance disbursement ceremony as the primary discovery candidate because PVB has disclosed relevant activity with Parañaque and BARMM.

This is not a production selection. Management must determine whether an active and suitable relationship exists and validate authority, funds, accounts, participants, conditions, channels, providers, evidence, exceptions, reconciliation, economics, and readiness.

Public collections remain the secondary candidate. Veterans' benefits and government or military payroll remain strategic later candidates.

## What Management must establish

Management should be prepared to answer five institutional questions:

1. **Purpose:** What institutional responsibility would PVB be evaluating, and how does it fit current strategy and risk appetite?
2. **Authority:** Which public entity and PVB authorities would govern the ceremony?
3. **Current state:** How do accounts, funds, participants, providers, systems, evidence, exceptions, and reconciliation operate today?
4. **Control:** Where do infrastructure, data, credentials, releases, providers, recovery, and production decisions reside?
5. **Readiness:** What evidence would PVB require before considering implementation or bounded production?

## Decision now

Authorize Management validation and require a separate return decision.

Do not authorize implementation, procurement, contracting, system access, integration, production, settlement activity, a public program, or commercial commitment.

The immediate task is to determine whether the accepted institutional question has a current, authorized, and sufficiently bounded basis within PVB.
