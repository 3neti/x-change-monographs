# Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program

## Executive Frequently Asked Questions

**Audience:** Board, President, Executive Committee, Strategy, Treasury, Government Relations, Digital Transformation, and Technology leadership

**Purpose:** Clarify the executive decision and its boundaries

**Canonical parent:** [Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program](../PHILIPPINE_VETERANS_BANK_MODERNIZATION_PROGRAM.md)

## 1. What decision is being requested now?

Whether Management should be authorized to validate the accepted Modernization Program and determine whether one bounded public-assistance disbursement ceremony is suitable for detailed design.

## 2. Why is the decision deliberately narrow?

The evidence establishes PVB's institutional relevance but leaves material questions about authority, current workflows, account structures, architecture, operating sovereignty, reconciliation, economics, and readiness. Validation must precede commitment.

## 3. Why is this relevant to PVB specifically?

PVB combines commercial-bank authority, a statutory veterans purpose, official government-depository status, and evidenced government, military, pensioner, LGU, assistance, collection, payment, and digital activity.

## 4. Is the Board being asked to approve implementation?

No. The requested decision authorizes institutional validation and a later return paper only.

## 5. Does the decision authorize procurement or a commercial agreement?

No. No price, provider appointment, procurement, contract, budget, or commercial commitment is part of this suite.

## 6. Does the Program give PVB new government-depository authority?

No. PVB's authority exists independently under the applicable legal and institutional framework. Each public workflow remains subject to its own lawful authority, selection, account, procurement, and operating requirements.

## 7. What institutional capability is being evaluated?

One maintained operating foundation relating authorized public instructions, recognized resources, service rules, participant conditions, execution, evidence, exceptions, and reconciliation.

## 8. Would public authority or banking responsibility move to a provider?

No. Government entities retain public-program authority and accountability. PVB retains banking authority, custody, accounts, operating control, settlement relationships, production decisions, records, and readiness.

## 9. Why is public-assistance disbursement the primary discovery candidate?

PVB has direct evidence of a student-allowance pilot with Parañaque and a financial-assistance relationship with BARMM. The domain is publicly important and may be bounded by authority, participants, value, duration, geography, and channel.

## 10. Has a public-assistance ceremony been selected for production?

No. It is a discovery candidate. Management must establish whether an active, authorized, strategically relevant, operationally familiar, and suitable basis exists.

## 11. What other ceremonies remain relevant?

Public collections are the secondary discovery candidate. Veterans' benefits and government or military payroll remain strategic later candidates. Treasury and GOCC services remain later institutional domains. Procurement settlement remains deferred pending direct PVB evidence.

## 12. What must Management validate about the candidate ceremony?

The public counterpart, authority, funds, accounts, custody, participants, data, conditions, channels, providers, execution, evidence, exceptions, reconciliation, support, security, economics, and readiness.

## 13. What does operating sovereignty mean in this decision?

PVB should retain ownership or control of its approved environment, institutional data, credentials, provider relationships, releases, monitoring, recovery, records, audit evidence, and production decisions. The public record does not establish the current state, so Management must validate it.

## 14. Does technical validation establish operational readiness?

No. Operational readiness requires evidence that people, policies, systems, accounts, funds, providers, support, accounting, reconciliation, security, continuity, and oversight can function as one accountable service.

## 15. What should Management return with?

A confirmed institutional purpose and sponsor; validated public and banking authority; current financial and regulatory conditions; a defined candidate ceremony and public counterpart; current-state and proposed boundaries; governance and sovereignty; preliminary economics; readiness dependencies; unresolved risks; and the exact next decision requested.

## 16. What happens if public assistance is not suitable?

Management should return to the evidence-supported candidate set rather than force selection. The accepted Program preserves public collections, veterans' benefits, payroll, Treasury, and GOCC domains under their stated evidence conditions.

## 17. Does this decision promise deposits, preferred status, revenue, or return?

No. Those outcomes depend on lawful selection, service design, financial standing, price, control quality, operating performance, and actual program structures.

## 18. What is the governing principle for the next step?

The first decision authorizes institutional learning. Every later decision must be supported by evidence appropriate to the responsibility PVB is being asked to carry.
