**Title:** Begin Where PVB Has Direct Evidence

**Evidence:** Parañaque student-allowance pilot and BARMM financial-assistance relationship.

**Boundary:** Discovery candidate, not production selection.

## Slide 7 — Other candidate domains

**Title:** Preserve the Wider Institutional Horizon

- Public collections — secondary discovery candidate.
- Veterans' benefits — strategic later candidate.
- Government or military payroll — strategic later candidate.
- Treasury and GOCC services — later institutional domain.
- Procurement settlement — deferred pending PVB-specific evidence.

## Slide 8 — Authority and operating sovereignty

**Title:** Responsibility Does Not Transfer

**Government retains:** Public purpose, program authority, participant basis, approvals, conditions, public accountability.

**PVB retains:** Banking authority, custody, accounts, environment, data, credentials, providers, settlement relationships, production decisions, evidence, readiness.

## Slide 9 — What Management must validate

**Title:** The Evidence Required for a Later Decision

Group the validation agenda under:

- strategy and authority;
- financial and regulatory capacity;
- public counterpart and ceremony;
- funds, accounts, participants, and conditions;
- technology, sovereignty, execution, and security;
- exceptions, reconciliation, evidence, and support; and
- economics and readiness.

## Slide 10 — The bounded authorization

**Title:** Authorize Learning, Require a Return Decision

**Decision requested:** Authorize Management validation.

**Required return:** Confirmed sponsor, authority, ceremony, boundaries, economics, readiness dependencies, unresolved risks, and exact next decision.

**Closing line:** Every later decision must be supported by evidence appropriate to the responsibility PVB is being asked to carry.
