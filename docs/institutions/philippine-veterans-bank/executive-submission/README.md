# Philippine Veterans Bank Executive Submission Suite

## Purpose

This suite is the executive communication layer derived from the accepted [Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program](../PHILIPPINE_VETERANS_BANK_MODERNIZATION_PROGRAM.md).

The Program remains the canonical institution-specific source. These publications explain its accepted institutional reasoning to distinct executive audiences. They do not replace, revise, or extend it.

## Executive decision

Every publication narrows the immediate decision to the same question:

> Should PVB authorize Management to validate the Modernization Program and determine whether one bounded public-assistance disbursement ceremony is suitable for detailed design?

That decision would authorize institutional learning. It would not authorize implementation, procurement, contracting, system access, integration, production, a public program, settlement activity, or a commercial agreement.

## Derivation rule

Every institutional proposition and recommendation must be traceable to the canonical Program. The suite may compress, sequence, or restate accepted content for an audience. It may not:

- introduce new evidence or institutional conclusions;
- change the selected discovery candidate;
- create new architecture or implementation priorities;
- imply PVB approval, readiness, or commitment;
- add commercial terms; or
- bypass the Program when future executive materials are prepared.

## Publication boundary

This governance refinement generates no PDF, presentation deck, proposal, financial brief, implementation plan, or commercial package. Board-ready publication generation is the next governed workstream and requires a separate production assignment. Commercial and implementation materials remain outside the suite's authority.
