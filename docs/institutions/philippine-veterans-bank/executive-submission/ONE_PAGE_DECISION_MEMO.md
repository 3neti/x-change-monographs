# Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program

## One-Page Decision Memo

**Audience:** Board of Directors and President

**Decision:** Authority for Management validation

**Canonical parent:** [Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program](../PHILIPPINE_VETERANS_BANK_MODERNIZATION_PROGRAM.md)

### Why this is before PVB

PVB is a private commercial bank, statutory veterans institution, and official government depository with evidenced public-sector, military, pensioner, assistance, collection, payment, and digital activity.

The accepted Program asks whether one reusable governed operating Capability could support selected public-finance services while PVB preserves banking authority, custody, accounts, data, providers, settlement relationships, production decisions, and readiness.

### Recommended decision

Authorize Management to:

1. validate the Program's institutional premise and material Unknowns;
2. determine whether one bounded public-assistance disbursement ceremony is current, authorized, strategically relevant, operationally familiar, and suitable for detailed design; and
3. return with evidence and the exact next decision requested.

### Why public assistance

PVB has disclosed a student-allowance pilot with Parañaque and a financial-assistance relationship with BARMM. These provide the strongest PVB-specific basis for examining public authority, participants, funds, conditions, execution, evidence, exceptions, and reconciliation within a bounded ceremony.

This is a discovery candidate—not a production selection.

### What Management must return

- institutional sponsor, purpose, authority, resources, and risk appetite;
- active public counterpart and validated ceremony;
- funds, accounts, custody, participants, conditions, and evidence;
- current systems, providers, interfaces, sovereignty, and controls;
- reconciliation, exception, support, security, and audit model;
- preliminary economics and current financial or regulatory dependencies;
- readiness conditions and unresolved risks; and
- a separate recommendation for any later step.

### What this decision does not authorize

No implementation. No procurement. No contract. No system access. No integration. No production. No public program. No settlement activity. No commercial commitment.

### Decision principle

The first decision authorizes institutional learning. Every later decision must be supported by evidence appropriate to the responsibility PVB is being asked to carry.
