# Philippine Veterans Bank Executive Submission Suite — Compass

## Governing question

> What should Philippine Veterans Bank's Board or Management decide after reading the accepted Modernization Program?

## Answer

The immediate decision is whether Management should be authorized to:

1. validate the institutional premise and material Unknowns in the Program;
2. determine whether one bounded public-assistance disbursement ceremony has a current, authorized, and suitable institutional basis; and
3. return with evidence and an exact next decision before any implementation commitment.

## Canonical parent

The [Philippine Veterans Bank Modernization Program](../PHILIPPINE_VETERANS_BANK_MODERNIZATION_PROGRAM.md) is read-only and authoritative.

The suite may explain the Program. It may not reinterpret it.

## Audience rule

Each publication serves a named executive audience. It should contain only the level of context that audience needs to understand the bounded decision.

Board-facing publications emphasize institutional purpose, authority, risk, readiness, and the decision requested. Functional audiences receive only enough operating context to understand what must be validated.

## Protected decisions

- The proposed institutional response is one reusable governed public-finance Capability.
- Bounded public-assistance disbursement is the primary discovery candidate.
- Public collections are the secondary candidate.
- Veterans' benefits and government or military payroll remain strategic later candidates.
- Treasury and GOCC services remain later institutional domains.
- Procurement settlement remains deferred pending PVB-specific evidence.
- Management validation precedes implementation.
- Readiness remains an institutional judgment.

## Prohibited drift

The suite must not introduce:

- a new first ceremony;
- new PVB facts or interpretations;
- technical architecture beyond the institutional boundary already accepted;
- prices, returns, schedules, or commitments;
- implementation or production authority; or
- a substitute for the canonical Program.

## Editorial benchmark

The AllBank Executive Edition may inform cadence, readability, completeness, and presentation discipline. PVB evidence, Program decisions, ceremony priorities, and conclusions remain controlling.

## Completion standard

The suite is complete for review when every publication:

1. names its audience and use;
2. identifies the canonical parent;
3. asks the same bounded executive decision;
4. preserves Unknowns and validation requirements;
5. excludes implementation and commercial authority; and
6. introduces no new institutional conclusion.
