# Evidence Register — Philippine Veterans Bank

## Classification key

- **Observed** — directly supported by an identified public source.
- **Inferred** — bounded interpretation of identified observations.
- **Recommended** — proposed action or application; reserved for later authorized work.
- **Unknown** — not established by available evidence.
- **Institution-Provided** — intentionally supplied under recorded use conditions; none used here.
- **Calculated** — derived transparently from cited inputs.

## Financial calculations

No calculated financial measure is used in the Phase 1 conclusions. Disclosed figures and ratios are reproduced with their source, period, unit, and reporting perimeter. This avoids creating unsupported comparability across audited consolidated statements, bank-only regulatory rankings, and published statements of condition.
