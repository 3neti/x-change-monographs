# Appendix F — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is PVB being invited to establish?

One reusable governed operating Capability through which selected public-finance services could be defined, executed, evidenced, and reconciled under PVB's authority and control.

## What does the immediate invitation authorize?

Nothing beyond institutional dialogue and, if PVB chooses, formal evaluation. It does not authorize procurement, implementation, a public program, system access, settlement activity, or production.

## Why begin with bounded public assistance?

PVB has relevant public-assistance experience, and a bounded ceremony can make the complete operating discipline visible within a limited institutional scope. Its present suitability, authority, economics, and readiness would still need to be confirmed with PVB and the responsible public institution.

## What if public assistance is not suitable?

PVB would return to the proposed candidate sequence rather than force selection. Public collections follow as the secondary candidate. Veterans' benefits and government or military payroll remain strategic later candidates. Treasury and GOCC services remain for later evaluation. Procurement settlement remains deferred pending direct institutional evidence.

## What does x-change do?

x-change coordinates the governed operating grammar beneath the banking service. It relates recognized resources, purpose, conditions, credential, execution, evidence, exceptions, and reconciliation.

## What is a Pay Code?

A Pay Code is a credential representing an authorized instruction. It is not money, a public benefit, a deposit, stored value, or the banking service.

## Where do public funds remain?

Within the applicable government and regulated PVB banking arrangements. x-change does not take custody. Treasury supplies an operational view and does not replace accounts or public accounting records.

## Who owns the government relationship?

PVB retains its direct government relationships. The government entity retains its public authority and accountability. Technology and service providers perform only their agreed roles.

## Who owns production?

PVB retains production authority for its banking service. Public-program production also requires the authority of the responsible government entity. No provider can authorize production on their behalf.

## Does common software create a shared bank?

No. PVB would retain its own environment, data, credentials, provider accounts, settlement relationships, records, and production decisions.

## Is a particular settlement provider required?

No exclusive provider is prescribed. PVB retains or establishes the applicable approved provider and settlement relationships.

## Is completed integration the same as readiness?

No. Integration and technical validation demonstrate agreed behavior under tested conditions. Readiness is the responsible institutions' judgment about the complete ceremony.

## Does the Program guarantee deposits, government selection, or revenue?

No. Those outcomes depend on lawful process, authority, program structure, qualification, price, service, evidence, and performance.

## What happens next?

PVB may determine whether the institutional question merits formal evaluation, whether bounded public assistance should test it first, and what evidence would be required before any later decision.
