# Appendix F — Frequently Asked Questions

## What is PVB being asked to approve?

The immediate decision is whether to authorize disciplined Management validation of one reusable governed public-finance Capability and determine whether a bounded public-assistance disbursement is suitable for detailed design. It is not an implementation or production approval.

## Why begin with public assistance?

PVB has disclosed a student-allowance pilot and a financial-assistance relationship, making public assistance the strongest evidence-led discovery candidate. [CLM-019, CLM-021] Its present status, authority, accounts, economics, controls, and readiness still require validation.

## What if public assistance is not suitable?

PVB should return to the evidence-supported candidate set rather than force selection. Public collections are the secondary candidate. Veterans' benefits and government or military payroll remain strategically relevant later candidates.

## What does x-change do?

x-change supplies the maintained operating grammar that relates authority, recognized resources, service rules, conditions, credential, execution, evidence, exceptions, and reconciliation. It does not become the bank or government entity.

## What is a Pay Code?

A Pay Code is a credential representing an authorized instruction. It is not money, a public benefit, a deposit, stored value, or the banking service.

## Where do public funds remain?

Within the applicable government and regulated banking arrangements. x-change does not take custody. Treasury supplies an operational view and does not replace PVB accounts or public accounting records.

## Who owns the government relationship?

PVB retains its direct institutional relationships. The government entity retains its public authority and accountability. Technology and service providers perform only their agreed roles.

## Who owns production?

PVB retains production authority for its banking service. Public-program production also requires the authority of the responsible government entity. No provider can authorize production on their behalf.

## Does common software create a shared bank?

No. PVB would retain a separate environment, data, credentials, provider accounts, settlement relationships, records, and production decisions.

## Is a specific settlement provider required?

No exclusive provider is prescribed. PVB retains or establishes the applicable approved provider and settlement relationships.

## Is completed integration the same as readiness?

No. Integration and technical certification demonstrate agreed behavior under tested conditions. Readiness is the responsible institutions' judgment about the complete ceremony.

## Does the Program guarantee deposits, government selection, or revenue?

No. Those outcomes depend on lawful process, authority, program structure, qualification, price, service, operating evidence, and performance.

## What must Management validate?

Strategy, sponsorship, authority, active public-finance relationships, funds and accounts, participants, systems, providers, sovereignty, controls, exceptions, reconciliation, economics, resources, and readiness.
