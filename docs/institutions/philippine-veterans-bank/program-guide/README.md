## Editorial boundary

The external manuscript and Appendices A–G contain no research stages, analytical lineage, claim identifiers, source registers, internal decisions, or repository workflow. Those materials remain internal governance scaffolding.

The main Guide speaks to institutional opportunity, the Program, the Capability, banking use cases, institutional value, and the launch path. The appendices preserve glossary, governance, economic grammar, Treasury grammar, deployment, FAQ, and diagrams.

The strongest evidence-supported first discovery candidate remains a bounded public-assistance disbursement ceremony, subject to Management Validation. The Guide does not authorize procurement, contracting, implementation, settlement activity, or production.
