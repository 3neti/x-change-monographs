## Editorial boundary

The external manuscript and Annexes A–H contain no research stages, analytical lineage, claim identifiers, source registers, internal decisions, or repository workflow. Those materials remain internal governance scaffolding.

The main Guide speaks to institutional opportunity, the Program, the Capability, banking use cases, institutional value, and the launch path. The annexes preserve the canonical operating model, ceremonies, institution-specific integration, governance, Treasury and settlement grammar, readiness, glossary, and application to PVB. Their architecture mirrors the AllBank publication while retaining Government Depository doctrine.

The strongest evidence-supported first discovery candidate remains a bounded public-assistance disbursement ceremony, subject to Management Validation. The Guide does not authorize procurement, contracting, implementation, settlement activity, or production.

## Change control

The publication identity, manuscript, section structure, Annexes A–H, candidate-ceremony ordering, doctrine, and visual treatment are closed. Subsequent changes require Version 1.1, a revised First Edition, or a later edition under explicit repository governance. Commercial Proposal, Cover Letter, and Demonstration Pay Codes remain separate workstreams.
