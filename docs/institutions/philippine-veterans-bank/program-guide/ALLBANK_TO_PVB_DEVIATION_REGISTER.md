# AllBank-to-PVB Deviation Register

## Purpose

This register records material institutional and doctrinal deviations made while adapting the approved AllBank Executive Edition into the Philippine Veterans Bank Executive Edition. It does not record routine name changes or sentence-level substitutions.

The AllBank Program Guide governs publication structure, voice, cadence, section movement, commercial posture, and scale. The accepted PVB corpus governs institution-specific accuracy. The Government Depository Program Guide governs public authority, custody, operating sovereignty, readiness, and public-finance doctrine.

## Deviation Register

### DEV-001 — Publication identity

**AllBank source function:** The title hierarchy identifies the sector Program Guide, institution edition, Executive Edition, and version.

**PVB adaptation:** `Philippine Veterans Bank` / `Digital Banking Program Guide` / `Government Depository Edition` / `Executive Edition` / `Version 1.0 — First Edition`.

**Reason:** PVB is the first institution-specific edition of the Government Depository Program Guide. Publication Freeze established an institution-first commercial hierarchy while retaining Government Depository lineage and the shared Program Guide identity.

**Support:** Government Depository Program Guide metadata; accepted PVB Modernization Program; current assignment.

**Classification:** Context substitution.

### DEV-002 — Institutional protagonist

**AllBank source function:** The Foreword and opening sections establish a thrift bank with customer, employer, merchant, SME, and lending relationships as the protagonist.

**PVB adaptation:** The same passages establish PVB as a private commercial bank, statutory veterans institution, and official government depository with veterans, military, public-sector, institutional, and commercial relationships.

**Reason:** PVB's three-part identity is the accepted basis of its institution-specific Program.

**Support:** CLM-001, CLM-004, CLM-005, CLM-011 through CLM-013, CLM-019 through CLM-023; Modernization Program Sections 1–3.

**Classification:** Context substitution.

### DEV-003 — Dual public and banking authority

**AllBank source function:** Section 2 assigns sponsorship, banking responsibility, provider roles, settlement relationships, and production authority.

**PVB adaptation:** Section 2 adds a separate paragraph for the responsible government entity and distinguishes public-program authority from PVB banking and production authority.

**Reason:** A government-depository ceremony requires public authority and banking authority to remain distinct. This creates the only additional prose paragraph relative to the AllBank Section 2 pattern.

**Support:** Government Depository Program Guide Sections 2 and 4; Modernization Program Sections 6 and 7.

**Classification:** Required doctrinal qualification.

### DEV-004 — Primary explanatory ceremony

**AllBank source function:** Remittance demonstrates the complete banking ceremony through one familiar request.

**PVB adaptation:** Bounded public-assistance disbursement demonstrates the complete public-finance ceremony through one familiar public responsibility.

**Reason:** The accepted PVB Modernization Program identifies bounded public-assistance disbursement as the primary discovery candidate.

**Support:** CLM-019, CLM-021; Modernization Program Section 5.1 and Appendix B.

**Classification:** Context substitution.

### DEV-005 — Candidate ceremony portfolio

**AllBank source function:** Payroll, merchant services, collections, housing or commercial releases, and remittance demonstrate the breadth of the reusable capability.

**PVB adaptation:** Public assistance, public collections, veterans' benefits, government or military payroll, Treasury and GOCC services, and procurement settlement demonstrate the PVB candidate sequence.

**Reason:** The PVB edition must preserve the accepted ceremony ordering and avoid importing AllBank priorities.

**Support:** Modernization Program Sections 5.1–5.4 and Appendix B.

**Classification:** Context substitution.

### DEV-006 — Relationship and Transaction Gravity context

**AllBank source function:** The Institutional Case relates payroll, merchants, collections, housing, and remittance to recurring customer and enterprise relationships.

**PVB adaptation:** The same movement relates assistance, public collections, veterans' benefits, government or military payroll, and Treasury to recurring public, constituent, and institutional relationships.

**Reason:** PVB's relationship opportunity arises from its veterans purpose, military constituencies, public-sector activity, and government-depository position rather than an AllBank customer-and-SME thesis.

**Support:** CLM-004, CLM-005, CLM-011 through CLM-013, CLM-019 through CLM-023; Modernization Program Sections 3 and 11.

**Classification:** Context substitution.

### DEV-007 — Integration terminology in the main body

**AllBank source function:** The main body introduces the common execution contract and bank-specific driver.

**PVB adaptation:** The main body uses `PVB-specific integration` and `institution-specific integration`; engineering realization remains in Annex C.

**Reason:** The approved Government Depository editorial boundary keeps implementation terminology out of the Board-facing narrative while preserving the same publication function and technical boundary.

**Support:** Government Depository Program Guide editorial-boundary review; Modernization Program Sections 4, 6, and 8.

**Classification:** Required doctrinal qualification.

### DEV-008 — Custody and public-fund qualification

**AllBank source function:** Section 4 states that funds remain within the bank's regulated relationships and x-change does not assume custody.

**PVB adaptation:** The passage distinguishes applicable government arrangements, PVB banking arrangements, government accounting records, accounts, and the general ledger.

**Reason:** Public-finance activity requires explicit separation of operational Treasury state from public authority, custody, fund character, and public accounting.

**Support:** Government Depository Program Guide Section 4 and Appendix D; Modernization Program Section 6 and Appendix E.

**Classification:** Required doctrinal qualification.

### DEV-009 — Launch sequence and first decision

**AllBank source function:** Section 7 invites dialogue to select the ceremony through which AllBank should examine the Capability.

**PVB adaptation:** Section 7 invites PVB to validate bounded public assistance first and return to the accepted candidate sequence if it is unsuitable.

**Reason:** The PVB Program already contains an accepted primary discovery candidate, while its current status and suitability remain subject to direct institutional validation.

**Support:** Modernization Program Sections 5.1, 9, 10, and 12.

**Classification:** Institution-specific addition.

### DEV-010 — Annex architecture alignment

**AllBank source function:** Eight in-manuscript annexes cover operating model, ceremonies, driver architecture, governance, Treasury, readiness, glossary, and application to AllBank.

**PVB adaptation:** Eight linked annexes follow the same journey: Canonical Operating Model, Banking Ceremonies, Institution Integration Architecture, Governance Principles, Treasury and Settlement Grammar, Institutional Readiness Framework, Glossary, and Applying This Program Guide to PVB.

**Reason:** Direct publication-family parity requires the PVB Executive Edition to follow the AllBank annex sequence. `Institution Integration Architecture` replaces `Bank Driver Architecture` as a contextual title so the executive publication preserves the Government Depository editorial boundary while retaining the same architectural function.

**Support:** Approved AllBank Executive Edition; Government Depository Program Guide doctrine; current assignment.

**Classification:** Matched structure with contextual substitution.

### DEV-011 — Internal research scaffolding removed from the publication

**AllBank source function:** The external AllBank publication states institutional context directly and contains no claim identifiers or research workflow narration.

**PVB adaptation:** The PVB manuscript and Annexes A–H state institution-specific context directly. Claim identifiers, research phases, gates, registers, and analytical lineage remain outside the external publication.

**Reason:** Internal evidence governance must inform the publication without appearing as part of its external voice.

**Support:** Current assignment; accepted PVB evidence and governance corpus.

**Classification:** Publication exception.

## Review Result

No material structural or tonal deviation remains unexplained. The main narrative and eight-annex journey now match the AllBank publication family; remaining differences are institutionally or doctrinally required. Routine institution-name changes and sentence-level adaptations are intentionally excluded from this register.
