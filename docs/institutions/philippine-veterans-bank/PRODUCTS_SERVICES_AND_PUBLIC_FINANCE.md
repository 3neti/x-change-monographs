# Philippine Veterans Bank — Products, Services, and Public-Finance Activity

## Scope

This note inventories publicly evidenced offerings and selected public-finance activity. Product presence does not establish adoption, economics, readiness, or technical architecture.

## Facts

### Deposits and customer accounts

- **Observed.** Disclosed products include individual, corporate, trust, and government savings, checking, and time-deposit accounts; foreign-currency accounts; pension and payroll accounts; Basic Deposit Accounts; and MDS-related account types. [CLM-012]
- **Observed.** The 2025 consolidated deposit base was ₱78.969 billion, comprising ₱47.073 billion demand, ₱21.586 billion savings, and ₱10.310 billion time deposits. [CLM-026, CLM-028]
- **Unknown.** Government deposit balances, concentration, sources, legal classifications, and account structures are not disclosed. [CLM-047]

### Lending

- **Observed.** Consumer lending includes housing, auto, salary, pension, emergency, educational, and other facilities. [CLM-011]
- **Observed.** Public-sector lending includes project and working-capital facilities for LGUs, NGAs, GOCCs, water districts, and electric cooperatives, with trade and guarantee facilities also listed. [CLM-013, CLM-037]
- **Observed.** The audited notes state that unsecured loans include salary loans to Department of Education and LGU employees and pension loans to veterans and AFP retirees, supported by salary or pension deduction arrangements. [SRC-004]

### Treasury, trust, and cash management

- **Observed.** PVB offers foreign exchange, government and corporate securities, and other Treasury services. [CLM-011]
- **Observed.** Trust offerings include investment management, employee-benefit trusts, trusteeship, escrow, agency, safekeeping, and personal-trust services. [SRC-004, SRC-006]
- **Observed.** Cash-management services include payroll, ATM installation, deposit pickup and cash delivery, automatic debit, checkwriting, POS, eGov and online tax-payment facilities, bills payment, and disbursement. [SRC-004, SRC-006]

### Payments, collections, and merchant services

- **Observed.** PVB identifies BancNet, PESONet, InstaPay, Bills Pay PH, POS, kiosks, debit cards, a payment gateway, and branch and digital bills-payment channels. [CLM-017]
- **Observed.** ECPay powers the disclosed kiosk service, and Paynamics is the identified payment-gateway provider. [CLM-018]
- **Observed.** The information statement says billers may receive real-time credit to a designated PVB settlement account under the described proprietary and Bills Pay PH arrangements. [SRC-006]
- **Unknown.** Public evidence does not establish provider contracts, operating-account structures, reversals, unresolved outcomes, or reconciliation evidence. [CLM-046, CLM-049]

### Government assistance and disbursement

- **Observed.** PVB describes BDA-based institutional disbursement as suitable for assistance to students, scholars, persons with disabilities, and senior citizens. [SRC-006]
- **Observed.** The 2025 annual report identifies a Parañaque pilot for electronic student-allowance disbursement. [CLM-019]
- **Observed.** A 2026 announcement records a BARMM agreement for financial-assistance disbursement and an offsite ATM. [CLM-021]
- **Observed.** PVB announced a payment kiosk at Malabon City Hall. [CLM-020]
- **Observed.** PVB disclosed a Province of Negros Occidental payment-gateway arrangement covering named provincial properties. [CLM-022]

### Military, veterans, payroll, and pensions

- **Observed.** Products include AFP and PVAO pension accounts and loans, AFP and LGU salary loans, company payroll accounts, ATM payroll, and services for veteran and other pensioner groups. [CLM-011–CLM-012]
- **Observed.** PVB disclosed an AFP memorandum of agreement and a strategic intention to deepen military-sector banking relationships. [CLM-023]

### Remittance and cross-border signals

- **Observed.** Direct remittance and OFW-related deposit products appear in the 2026 inventory; foreign exchange and trade-remittance capabilities are also disclosed. [SRC-006]
- **Unknown.** Current remittance partners, corridors, transaction volumes, customer journey, settlement arrangements, and economics are not established.

## Observed implications

- **Inferred.** PVB has publicly evidenced capabilities across both sides of public financial activity: receiving and managing institution relationships, and executing collections or disbursements through multiple channels. [CLM-013, CLM-017–CLM-022, CLM-043]
- **Inferred.** The BDA provides a visible connection between a public program, an individually held account, and beneficiary access. The evidence does not show the full governance and exception model beneath that connection. [CLM-019, CLM-049]
- **Inferred.** PVB's military and pension products create a relationship base that differs from a generic government-depository proposition. The size and current utilization of that base remain Unknown. [CLM-011, CLM-023]
