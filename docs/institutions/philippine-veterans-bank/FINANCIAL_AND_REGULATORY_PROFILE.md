## Audit opinion and going-concern disclosure

- **Observed.** P&A Grant Thornton issued a qualified opinion on the 2025 consolidated and parent-company financial statements. [SRC-004]
- **Observed.** One qualification concerned deferred impairment on an old thrift-bank exposure. The auditor stated that the effect would increase deficit and reduce assets and equity by ₱0.40 billion at 31 December 2025. [SRC-004]
- **Observed.** A second matter concerned the use of a 2023 rather than 2025 land appraisal and the absence of a related deferred tax liability. The auditor described the estimated balance-sheet effects. [SRC-004]
- **Observed.** The auditor separately identified a material uncertainty related to going concern. [SRC-004]

## Capital and regulatory context

- **Observed.** The bank's CAR at 31 December 2025 included an appraisal increment. The auditor stated that without it PVB would not have complied with the minimum regulatory capitalization and CAR requirements. [CLM-031]
- **Observed.** The disclosed CBUP remained dependent on capital raising, investor support, and timely BSP approvals. Revised plans and extension requests remained under regulatory review as described in the report. [SRC-004]
- **Observed.** Certain other BSP requirements were not met, and monetary and non-monetary sanctions were disclosed. [SRC-004]
- **Observed.** PVB also reported high LCR and NSFR and a return to material profitability. [CLM-027, CLM-030]

## Capital structure distinctions

The following must not be treated as equivalent:

- ₱10 billion authorized capital stock under the Revised Charter;
- audited consolidated equity of ₱10.843 billion at 31 December 2025;
- parent and consolidated qualifying capital used for CAR;
- paid-in capital stock;
- deposits for future stock subscriptions;
- appraisal increment; and
- a public announcement that a capitalization target had been achieved.

The audited statements and regulatory approvals govern the relevant interpretation. [SRC-004, SRC-006, SRC-013]

## Observed implications

- **Inferred.** PVB entered 2026 with stronger earnings, deposits, assets, and disclosed liquidity than in 2024. [CLM-026–CLM-034]
- **Inferred.** The institution still faced material capital, audit, and regulatory dependencies that may affect sequencing, risk appetite, and investment capacity. [CLM-030–CLM-031]
- **Inferred.** Aggregate financial growth cannot establish the economics or operating success of government-depository services. [CLM-047]
