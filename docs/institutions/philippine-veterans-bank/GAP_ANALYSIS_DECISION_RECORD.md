# Philippine Veterans Bank Gap Analysis Decision Record

## Purpose

This record preserves the methodological decisions used to compare the evidenced Philippine Veterans Bank with the canonical Government Depository institutional capability. It records classification and evidence-handling decisions. It does not record institutional recommendations, Program choices, or implementation decisions.

## Status model

- **Accepted for Analysis** — applied in the Gate 2 Gap Analysis;
- **Open** — requires a later editorial or management decision; and
- **Superseded** — replaced by a later recorded decision.

## Decision register

### GAD-001 — Treat Gate 1 approval as comparison authority only

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Gate 1 approval authorizes comparison with the canonical framework. It does not establish PVB interest, Program authorization, or readiness.

**Rationale:** Editorial authority to perform analysis is distinct from institutional authority to evaluate or implement a Program.

**Consequence:** CLM-048 and UNK-010 remain open, and no recommendation or executive action is advanced.

### GAD-002 — Require traceability on both sides of every comparison

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Each material finding must identify PVB evidence and the canonical concept against which it is compared.

**Rationale:** A comparison is incomplete if either the institutional state or canonical baseline is assumed.

**Consequence:** The matrix cites internal claim IDs and the applicable Guide, appendix, or readiness source.

### GAD-003 — Do not classify absence of public evidence as a gap

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Use Unknown or Management Validation Required when public evidence cannot establish the current institutional state.

**Rationale:** Non-public architecture, operating controls, or performance may exist. Their absence from public sources is not evidence of institutional absence.

**Consequence:** Reconciliation, exception management, deployment ownership, and readiness remain Unknown rather than deficient.

### GAD-004 — Use Proposed Program Capability for canonical grammar not evidenced at PVB

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Classify Funding Events, Settlement Resources as canonical operational state, Liens, Facilities, Settlement Envelopes, Pay Codes, and reusable operating grammar as Proposed Program Capabilities where PVB evidence is insufficient.

**Rationale:** These capabilities are defined by the canonical Program. The evidence does not establish them as PVB's current model, but also does not prove that no analogous controls exist.

**Consequence:** The analysis avoids the words missing and absent as institutional findings.

### GAD-005 — Separate banking authority from public-program authority

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Treat public purpose, appropriation, beneficiary entitlement, and program approval as outside PVB's banking authority.

**Rationale:** This is the canonical responsibility boundary and is consistent with PVB's evidence as a bank and government depository.

**Consequence:** Outside Current Authority is used as a correct boundary, not a weakness.

### GAD-006 — Separate payment capability from governed execution capability

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Treat PVB's visible rails, channels, providers, gateways, kiosks, and disbursement activity as partial evidence of execution, not as proof of the complete canonical Capability.

**Rationale:** Connectivity does not establish authority, conditions, operational state, exception handling, evidence, and reconciliation.

**Consequence:** Execution is Partially Evidenced rather than Aligned or missing.

### GAD-007 — Separate Treasury technology from canonical Treasury visibility

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Treat PVB's Treasury services and disclosed Guava system as a meaningful signal, not proof of canonical Funding Event, Settlement Resource, Lien, or ceremony-state behavior.

**Rationale:** A named system does not establish its architecture, coverage, data, controls, or relationship to public-finance workflows.

**Consequence:** Treasury visibility is Partially Evidenced; its canonical components remain proposed or require validation.

### GAD-008 — Assess evidence confidence separately from capability quality

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Confidence describes how firmly the evidence supports the classification, not whether PVB performs well.

**Rationale:** Low confidence often reflects a public-evidence boundary rather than weak institutional performance.

**Consequence:** The matrix distinguishes confidence in known institutional facts from confidence in non-public operating state.

### GAD-009 — Preserve readiness as a sequence of institutional judgments

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Do not infer later readiness levels from institution assessment readiness, digital launches, provider connections, regulatory applications, or past pilots.

**Rationale:** The canonical model prohibits readiness-stage skipping.

**Consequence:** Program, workflow, implementation, driver, operational, limited-production, production, and expansion readiness remain Unknown or require management validation.

### GAD-010 — Evaluate ceremonies without selection

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Compare veterans benefits, government payroll, LGU and public assistance, public collections, Treasury and GOCC activity, and procurement settlement only for evidence-supported relevance.

**Rationale:** Ceremony selection belongs to the Modernization Program and requires institutional authority, priority, bounded scope, operating familiarity, and readiness.

**Consequence:** No first ceremony is recommended.

### GAD-011 — Preserve Transaction Gravity as a tendency

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Recognize recurring relationship surfaces without predicting deposits, loyalty, preferred status, or commercial return.

**Rationale:** The public record does not establish customer-level longitudinal behavior or cross-product outcomes.

**Consequence:** Transaction Gravity remains a bounded analytical observation.

### GAD-012 — Keep AllBank outside Gate 2

**Status:** Accepted for Analysis

**Date:** 20 July 2026

**Decision:** Do not consult the AllBank Program during Gap Analysis.

**Rationale:** It is authorized only as an editorial benchmark after the first complete PVB Modernization Program draft.

**Consequence:** No AllBank content, assumptions, classifications, or publication structure entered the analysis.
