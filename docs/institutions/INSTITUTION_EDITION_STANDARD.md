# Institution Edition Standard

## Purpose

An Institution Edition applies a canonical Program Guide to a specific institution.

It does not create new doctrine. It makes the canonical framework institutionally legible by adapting examples, priorities, emphasis, language, customer journeys, and rollout sequence within approved boundaries.

## Required lineage

Every Institution Edition must identify:

- the governing Institutional Study or sector doctrine;
- the canonical Program Guide and source edition;
- the accepted Institution Intelligence version;
- the reviewed Strategic Gap Analysis version;
- the accepted Editorial Mapping version; and
- relevant Editorial Decision Records.

If the lineage is incomplete, drafting must stop.

## What may adapt

An Institution Edition may adapt:

- customer and institutional examples;
- banking-ceremony emphasis;
- business priorities;
- implementation emphasis;
- institutionally familiar language;
- customer journeys;
- governance emphasis;
- rollout sequence; and
- institution-specific questions supported by evidence.

## What must remain canonical

The edition must preserve:

- governing doctrine;
- architecture and Program boundaries;
- canonical operating grammar;
- custody and authority boundaries;
- software and settlement responsibilities;
- readiness as an institutional judgment;
- the distinction between banking service, Banking Ceremony, and credential;
- the distinction between Program and enduring capability; and
- all other frozen propositions recorded by the parent Program Guide and Editorial Decision Register.

## Evidence rule

Institution-specific factual claims must remain traceable to the approved evidence base. The manuscript may compress research; it may not improve certainty.

Recommendations should be framed as proposed applications, not existing institutional intentions. Material evidence changes require revalidation of the mapping and affected manuscript sections.

## Architecture rule

An Institution Edition inherits the canonical Program Guide's required business questions and mandatory coverage. It may rebalance emphasis but may not silently remove a section or coverage category.

If institutional relevance appears to require new doctrine or a structural deviation, record the issue and seek an Editorial Decision Record before adopting it.

## Relationship to proposals

An Institution Edition explains institutional applicability. It does not establish final scope, price, implementation commitment, legal terms, or production authorization.

Commercial proposals follow the edition. They do not belong inside it unless the canonical publication architecture expressly reserves a high-level commercial explanation.

## Review standard

Review the edition for:

- doctrinal fidelity;
- evidence fidelity;
- canonical parity;
- institution-specific relevance;
- clear separation of observation and recommendation;
- restrained institutional tone;
- absence of unsupported architecture;
- absence of proposal terms; and
- traceability to the accepted Editorial Mapping.

## Lifecycle

Institution Editions may evolve through field use. Each edition must record its source lineage, status, version, and superseding relationship.

Field observations may improve a later institution edition. They do not change canonical doctrine unless approved through editorial governance.
