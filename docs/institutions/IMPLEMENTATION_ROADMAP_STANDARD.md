# Implementation Roadmap Standard

## Purpose

The Implementation Roadmap describes how approved institution-specific work may progress from discovery through controlled expansion.

This standard is conceptual. A roadmap is not a production authorization, guaranteed schedule, technical design, or substitute for the institution's governance.

## Canonical progression

```text
Discovery
   ↓
Institution understanding
   ↓
Driver discovery
   ↓
Implementation
   ↓
Pilot
   ↓
Expansion
```

## Phase 1 — Discovery

Confirm scope, authorities, participants, intended Banking Ceremony, customer and operating context, settlement relationships, evidence needs, and material constraints.

Outputs may include confirmed questions, source and access requirements, responsibility assignments, and decision gates.

## Phase 2 — Institution understanding

Validate the operating reality that public Institution Intelligence could not establish. Confirm product ownership, policies, customer journeys, approval authorities, evidence, reconciliation, support, risk, compliance, technology boundaries, and readiness responsibilities.

Institution-provided discovery may refine the institution-specific edition or proposal. It does not silently rewrite canonical doctrine.

## Phase 3 — Driver discovery

Determine the institution-specific integration requirement.

Cover, where applicable:

- relevant APIs and documentation;
- authentication and credentials;
- settlement and status models;
- limits and error handling;
- reconciliation and evidence;
- environment access;
- certification expectations;
- dependency ownership; and
- maintenance responsibility.

The output is a validated discovery basis. It is not a completed driver.

## Phase 4 — Implementation

Implement the approved institution-specific scope, which may include driver engineering, configuration, deployment, integration, controls, customer experience, evidence, monitoring, training, and operating procedures.

Technical completion must remain distinct from operational readiness.

## Phase 5 — Pilot

Operate one bounded, approved Banking Ceremony under defined participants, limits, conditions, evidence, support, reconciliation, and governance.

The pilot should conclude with an institutional decision such as:

> Proceed • Correct • Extend • Pause

Pilot evidence informs the decision. Expansion is not automatic.

## Phase 6 — Expansion

Consider wider participation, additional customers, channels, branches, ceremonies, integrations, or operating capacity only after the institution reviews pilot evidence and confirms readiness.

Reuse the governed operating foundation where appropriate. Do not assume that every new service is merely a technical configuration.

## Required roadmap fields

For each phase, record:

- objective;
- entry criteria;
- activities;
- accountable institution authority;
- provider responsibilities;
- dependencies;
- evidence or deliverables;
- exit criteria;
- decision owner;
- principal risks; and
- unresolved questions.

## Readiness rule

Readiness is an institutional judgment, not a technical declaration.

Driver certification, successful deployment, passing tests, provider confirmation, or settlement connectivity may be necessary. None alone authorizes production.

## Change control

Roadmaps evolve as discovery improves understanding. Record material changes to scope, sequencing, responsibilities, dependencies, and gates.

Do not rewrite earlier intelligence or analysis merely because implementation reveals new facts. Update and supersede the affected artifacts visibly, then revalidate downstream work.
