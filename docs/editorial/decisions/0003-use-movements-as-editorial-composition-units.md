# 0003 — Use Movements as Editorial Composition Units

## Status

Accepted

## Context

The Founder's Discovery Thesis is not merely divided into chapters.

Each major section behaves like a movement of thought with its own opening, development, pivot, insight, and cadence.

## Decision

Use movements as the primary unit of editorial composition.

## Consequences

- Editorial composition happens movement by movement.
- Page composition happens later.
- Typography does not determine editorial rhythm.
- Each movement can be reviewed and approved independently.
