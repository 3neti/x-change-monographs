# 0002 — Separate Author Manuscript, Editorial Manuscript, and Publication Master

## Status

Accepted

## Context

A monograph passes through several forms before publication.

Confusing these forms makes future revision difficult.

## Decision

Separate the following artifacts:

- Author Manuscript
- Editorial Manuscript
- Publication Master
- Released Edition

## Consequences

- The author's original manuscript remains preserved.
- The editorial manuscript becomes the canonical literary source.
- Publication masters can be regenerated.
- Visual design changes do not require rewriting the manuscript.
