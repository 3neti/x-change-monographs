# Annex F — Institution-Specific Research Gate

## Purpose

This gate prevents sector doctrine from being mistaken for evidence about a named institution. No Philippine Veterans Bank Institution Edition, proposal, implementation roadmap, or institution-specific submission package is authorized by the Program foundation.

## Canonical Evidence Rule

Institution Intelligence must distinguish:

- **Observed** — directly supported by a cited public source or authorized institution-provided evidence;
- **Inferred** — a reasoned interpretation of observed evidence, clearly identified as inference;
- **Recommended** — a proposed course of action, not a statement of current fact;
- **Unknown** — material information not established by available evidence; and
- **Institution-Provided** — information supplied by the institution and governed according to its source and use restrictions.

If sufficient evidence is unavailable, the research agent must identify the gap rather than compensate with unsupported prose.

## Philippine Veterans Bank Research Dossier

Future research should examine, at minimum and only through authorized evidence:

- the revised charter and applicable amendments;
- annual reports, audited financial statements, and material public disclosures;
- ownership, governance, Board, executive leadership, and institutional mandate;
- stated strategy, priorities, modernization initiatives, and public commitments;
- digital channels, products, customer segments, and service footprint;
- government-depository authority and publicly evidenced government-depository activity;
- branch, service, and geographic footprint;
- publicly disclosed government clients, partnerships, and relationships;
- the veterans, retirees, pensioners, families, and related institutional ecosystem;
- deposit, lending, payments, collections, disbursement, payroll, merchant, treasury, and public-finance products;
- settlement, clearing, payment-system, technology, cloud, cybersecurity, API, and outsourcing disclosures;
- speeches, interviews, press releases, procurement notices, partnership announcements, and strategic disclosures;
- relevant public materials from the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas, Department of Finance, Bureau of the Treasury, Department of Budget and Management, Commission on Audit, Social Security System, Government Service Insurance System, Department of National Defense, and Philippine Veterans Affairs Office; and
- public procurement, contractual, implementation, or performance evidence relevant to a proposed engagement.

This list is a research route, not an assertion that each source exists, is public, or supports a particular conclusion.

## Gate Deliverables

Institution-specific drafting may begin only after the authorized research package contains:

1. a source register with dates, provenance, access information, and source quality;
2. a completed Institution Intelligence record using the canonical framework;
3. an evidence ledger separating Observed, Inferred, Recommended, Unknown, and Institution-Provided material;
4. a Strategic Gap Analysis distinguishing institutional need from product assumption;
5. an Editorial Mapping record showing how evidence may enter an Institution Edition without changing doctrine;
6. an architecture-boundary review for authority, custody, settlement, deployment, driver, evidence, and readiness claims; and
7. an explicit editorial decision authorizing the next institution-specific artifact.

## Prohibited Shortcuts

The following do not satisfy the gate:

- relying on Movement IV's intended first-reader positioning as evidence of institutional commitment;
- treating charter authority as proof of present operating capability;
- treating a website product description as proof of implementation readiness;
- treating payment connectivity as a complete banking service;
- inferring government relationships, customer counts, system architecture, APIs, account structures, or pricing without evidence;
- copying facts from another bank's Institution Edition; or
- drafting a proposal before the responsible institutional, legal, technical, operational, and commercial questions have been established.

## Gate Outcome

The gate may produce **Proceed**, **Proceed with stated unknowns**, **Pause for institution-provided evidence**, or **Do not proceed**. It must never manufacture certainty merely to advance the publication sequence.
