# Annex G — Definition of Institution Readiness

## Purpose

Institution readiness is not one binary technical state. It is a sequence of decisions made by the institutions responsible for doctrine, research, authority, risk, operations, and public accountability.

## Readiness Levels

| Readiness level | Question answered | Minimum evidence | Decision owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sector-foundation readiness | Is the reusable sector field defined without changing canonical doctrine? | Program specification, parity report, compass, roadmap, complete foundation annexes, and navigation | Editorial governance |
| Formal publication readiness | Does the Program Guide answer every EDR-005 business question and mandatory coverage category? | Approved Executive Summary, Sections 1–7, Appendices A–G, parity review, metadata, QA, and freeze record | Editorial governance |
| Research readiness | Is a named-institution research workstream authorized and sufficiently scoped? | Research question, source policy, roles, evidence classifications, and institution boundary | Editorial and research governance |
| Institution-assessment readiness | Is there enough reliable evidence to describe the institution without unsupported claims? | Institution Intelligence, source register, evidence ledger, unknowns, and Strategic Gap Analysis | Research and editorial governance |
| Institution-edition readiness | Can approved doctrine be mapped to the institution without introducing new doctrine? | Editorial Mapping, claim audit, architecture-boundary review, and approval | Editorial governance |
| Engagement readiness | Is there enough institutional context and authority to discuss a bounded engagement? | Named stakeholders, problem, scope, authority, dependencies, unknowns, and approved engagement path | Participating institutions |
| Technical-discovery readiness | Can institution-specific systems and interfaces be examined responsibly? | Authorized access, architecture scope, security constraints, provider contacts, and discovery plan | Institution technology and security authorities |
| Driver-certification readiness | Has the institution-specific driver met its defined technical and provider criteria? | API mapping, implementation, test evidence, error handling, security review, provider evidence, and maintenance plan | Institution and relevant providers |
| Operational-readiness assessment | Can people, policies, controls, accounts, funds, data, providers, support, reconciliation, evidence, continuity, and exceptions operate together? | Approved operating design and cross-functional evidence | Institution management and authorized control functions |
| Limited-production readiness | Should one bounded live ceremony proceed under agreed controls? | Scope, approvals, participant treatment, limits, monitoring, support, rollback or pause criteria, and pre-agreed evidence | Responsible institution and public-program authorities |
| Production readiness | Should the institution assume continuing responsibility for the approved service? | Satisfactory pilot evidence, unresolved-risk decisions, legal and commercial instruments, capacity, controls, continuity, and accountable approval | Board or delegated institutional authority, as applicable |
| Expansion readiness | Should the institution add a workflow, population, geography, channel, provider, or volume? | Evidence from current operation and an approved change assessment | Responsible institutional authorities |

## Canonical Distinctions

- Software availability is not institution readiness.
- Settlement connectivity is not a banking service.
- Driver certification is not operational readiness.
- A successful demonstration is not production evidence.
- Board approval may begin a program; it does not place an institution into production.
- Readiness is an institutional judgment, not a technical declaration.

## Current Government Depository Status

The sector foundation becomes complete when the Phase 1 deliverables pass verification. The formal Program Guide, program publications, named-institution research, Institution Edition, proposal, and implementation remain separate future gates.

Philippine Veterans Bank has not been assessed by this foundation. No statement in the Program specification or annexes should be interpreted as a finding that the bank is research-ready, engagement-ready, technically ready, operationally ready, or selected for implementation.
