# Government Depository Digital Banking Program Compass

## Purpose

The Government Depository Digital Banking Program helps trusted government depository institutions administer public money more effectively through accountable, institution-controlled, digitally coordinated financial infrastructure.

Its emotional center is **public trust**. Its program product is **institutional capability**. Its strategic outcome is to enable eligible and trusted institutions to become stronger or preferred institutional options for selected public-finance workflows, subject always to authority, qualification, selection, agreement, implementation, evidence, and performance.

## Canonical Architecture

The Program applies the same canonical operating grammar used across the x-change corpus:

```text
Public authority and institutional instruction
                    ↓
Funding Event
                    ↓
Treasury and Lien
                    ↓
Facility
                    ↓
Settlement Envelope
                    ↓
Pay Code or approved presentation
                    ↓
Execution Engine and institution-specific driver
                    ↓
Approved settlement or service provider
                    ↓
Evidence, reconciliation, exception handling, and audit
```

The institution brings the authority, accounts, relationships, controls, and approved rails. x-change brings the maintained operating grammar. The institution-specific driver connects that grammar to the institution's approved providers and systems. Common software does not create a common institution.

## Known Risks

- confusing Movement IV doctrine with a complete implementation Guide;
- creating a Government Depository fork of the canonical architecture;
- presenting public-finance opportunity as statutory authority or procurement preference;
- treating charter language or public positioning as evidence of current capability;
- promising deposits or transaction volume as a licensing result;
- drafting PVB material before sufficient evidence exists;
- duplicating renderers, metadata, submission builders, or freeze mechanisms;
- allowing technical certification to stand in for institutional readiness; and
- losing traceability between canonical source and executive or commercial derivatives.

## Program Governance

The Program is governed by:

- Movement IV for institutional doctrine;
- the Canonical Banking Program Guide for formal Guide structure and mandatory coverage;
- the Editorial Decision Register for accepted architecture and publication decisions;
- canonical Treasury, Pay Code, execution, custody, settlement, licensing, and operating-sovereignty doctrine;
- the Institution Intelligence Framework for named-institution evidence;
- the Submission Architecture for future executive portfolios; and
- immutable versioning and freeze records for accepted publication editions.

Sector-specific language may adapt public purpose, stakeholders, workflows, legal context, audience emphasis, and institutional economics. It may not casually redefine canonical architecture or present a named-institution inference as fact.
