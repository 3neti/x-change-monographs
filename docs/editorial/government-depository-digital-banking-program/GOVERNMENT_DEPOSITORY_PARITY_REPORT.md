## Executive Verdict

Government Depository documentation is **mostly aligned at editorial-source level** with the completed Thrift Bank publishing stack. It has not reached publication parity.

The institutional doctrine is mature. [Movement IV](../movement-iv-government-depository-banking/modernizing-public-finance-through-trusted-government-depository-banking.md) is an approved and published white paper with a clear public-trust thesis, an institution-first model, and explicit operating-sovereignty boundaries. The shared repository already supplies the canonical Program Guide architecture, Treasury grammar, Institution Intelligence methodology, publication governance, demonstration-credential grammar, and Submission Portfolio architecture.

The approved Program foundation closed the sector-definition gap. The editorially complete Version 0.9 Program Guide closes the formal-architecture and mandatory-coverage gap. It mirrors the canonical seven-question structure, includes Guide Appendices A–G, and preserves the shared diagrams and operating grammar. Its synchronized PDF and HTML form the active Editorial Review Proof; publication freeze remains pending.

The accurate status is therefore:

- **architectural alignment:** established;
- **institutional thesis:** established and frozen in Movement IV;
- **program foundation:** approved;
- **formal Program Guide:** editorially complete canonical Version 0.9 review baseline;
- **program publication stack:** synchronized Editorial Review Proof active; publication freeze pending;
- **institution-specific implementation:** Philippine Veterans Bank research planned but not started; and
- **overall publishing parity with the Thrift Bank track:** not yet achieved.

## Risks

| Risk | Consequence | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Duplication | Separate renderers, schemas, or architectures become inconsistent | Reuse before create; record any genuinely new shared component |
| Publication drift | Derivatives change doctrine or responsibility boundaries | Maintain source-to-derivative traceability and parity review |
| Unsupported institutional claims | Named institutions are portrayed as possessing priorities, relationships, or capability without evidence | Enforce Annex F and the Institution Intelligence evidence classes |
| Legal ambiguity | Program prose is mistaken for authority, eligibility, procurement preference, or legal advice | Keep all outcomes conditional and require institution/legal validation |
| Premature PVB assumptions | First-reader positioning is treated as institutional evidence or commitment | Defer all PVB work to a separately authorized research-first phase |
| Architectural drift | Public-sector terminology creates a second Pay Code, voucher, Treasury, settlement, or execution model | Apply the no-fork invariants and require an EDR for doctrine changes |
| Commercial overreach | Deposits, transaction activity, or preferred-institution positioning are presented as guaranteed | Treat them as conditional consequences of successful relationships and performance |
| Readiness compression | Technical success is mistaken for institutional or production readiness | Preserve the separate readiness levels in Annex G |
