# Banking Program Guides

This directory contains the canonical architecture, parity control, and sector implementations for x-change banking Program Guides.

- [Canonical Banking Program Guide](CANONICAL_BANKING_PROGRAM_GUIDE.md) defines the reusable commercial-handbook architecture and agent obligations.
- [Banking Program Guide Parity Checklist](PROGRAM_GUIDE_PARITY_CHECKLIST.md) records mandatory coverage across sector guides.
- [RBAP Digital Banking Program Guide](rbap/rbap-digital-banking-program-guide.md) is the first implementation. Its Executive Summary was revalidated and locked under EDR-012 without wording changes. Sections 1–7 and Appendices A–G remain canonical and locked, including the Appendix G Mermaid sources. The complete editorial source is canonical and locked. [Edition 1](../../../build/pdf/rbap-digital-banking-program-guide-edition-1.pdf) remains the unchanged first-freeze Board paper; final publication governance and rendering remain pending.
- [Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide](thrift-bank/README.md) is complete under EDR-013 with two canonical publications: the concise [Executive Edition](../../../build/pdf/thrift-bank-digital-banking-program-guide-executive-edition-v1.pdf) and the comprehensive [Institutional Reference Edition](../../../build/pdf/thrift-bank-digital-banking-program-guide-institutional-reference-edition-v1.pdf). Neither supersedes the other. The [Editorial Acceptance](thrift-bank/EDITORIAL_ACCEPTANCE.md) and approved Institutional Study remain authoritative for frozen doctrine; future work proceeds through institution-specific editions.
- [Government Depository Digital Banking Program Guide](government-depository/README.md) is a complete Version 0.9 draft under review. It implements the EDR-005 structure and all mandatory coverage from the approved [Program foundation](../government-depository-digital-banking-program/README.md). Coverage is complete for parity; the manuscript and appendices are not editorially locked or approved for publication.

Program Guides are separate from sector white papers. White papers establish strategic thought leadership; Program Guides explain a program's institutional proposition, operating model, business case, and launch path.

Generated publication files belong under `build/`, not in this directory.

Future institution-specific editions must begin with the [Institution Intelligence Framework](../../institutions/README.md), canonical and locked under [EDR-014](../decisions/EDITORIAL_DECISION_REGISTER.md#edr-014--institution-intelligence-framework-is-canonical). Institution Intelligence, Strategic Gap Analysis, and Editorial Mapping remain separate from the canonical Program Guide and must precede institution-specific drafting.
