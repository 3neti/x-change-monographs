# Appendix D — Treasury Grammar

Treasury gives the institution a consistent operational view of financial state across a governed public-finance ceremony. The grammar distinguishes recognized resources, reservations, service meaning, conditions, execution, and reconciliation without changing the authority or custody beneath them.

## Canonical Progression

```text
External financial event
        ↓
Funding Event
        ↓
Settlement Resources recognized by Treasury
        ↓
Lien reserves resources for an authorized purpose
        ↓
Facility gives the activity banking, public-service, and approved commercial meaning
        ↓
Settlement Envelope establishes evidence and conditions
        ↓
Pay Code or approved presentation represents the authorized instruction
        ↓
Execution coordinates the approved outcome
        ↓
Evidence, state resolution, and reconciliation
```

## Governing Distinctions

- A Funding Event records an external event; it does not appropriate, authorize, or manufacture public funds.
- Treasury represents operational state; it is not the government account, bank account, appropriation record, trust record, or general ledger.
- Settlement Resources retain their applicable legal, public, custody, and accounting character outside the operational grammar.
- A Lien reserves capacity; it does not transfer custody.
- A Facility carries service and approved commercial meaning; a Pay Code does not.
- The governing government program remains the source of public purpose and authority; a Facility does not replace it.
- A Settlement Envelope carries conditions and evidence; it does not perform settlement.
- A Pay Code is a credential; it is not money, a benefit, or the service itself.
- A voucher may present an approved instruction; it does not create a separate balance or execution model.
- Execution coordinates the authorized outcome through the institution's approved capability.
- Reconciliation compares operating state with public, banking, and provider evidence and resolves differences through approved procedures.

Throughout this progression, the underlying funds remain within applicable government and regulated banking relationships. The grammar makes operating state visible; it does not change custody or public authority.
