# Appendix B — Governance

Governance follows public and institutional responsibility. No provider approval substitutes for the approval of the responsible government entity or depository institution.

| Participant | Principal responsibility | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Board of Directors | Approves institutional purpose, risk appetite, scope, accountability, and material progression | Board approval begins the Program; it does not confer public authority or establish production readiness |
| President / CEO / Management | Owns Program sponsorship, resources, accountability, and management decisions | Does not treat Program authorization as government-program approval |
| Government entity / authorized officials | Establish or administer public purpose, authority, funding basis, participants, conditions, approvals, reporting, and public accountability | Do not transfer public authority to the bank or a technology provider |
| Business / public-sector banking | Defines the depository banking service, institutional responsibility, relationship model, terms, and economic case | Does not assign public authority or commercial meaning to a credential |
| Operations | Defines supervision, participant support, escalation, exception handling, evidence, and reconciliation practice | Does not rely on technical completion as an operating decision |
| Treasury | Governs operational financial state, reservations, liquidity, provider positions, and unresolved obligations | x-change Treasury does not replace accounts or determine legal fund character |
| Technology | Governs architecture, integration, environments, identity, observability, resilience, service management, and release | External engineering extends rather than displaces institutional capability |
| Risk and Compliance | Reviews risks, controls, obligations, monitoring, financial-crime controls, and participant treatment within authority | Does not transfer accountability to certification artifacts |
| Legal | Reviews authority, agreements, procurement or engagement, data treatment, terms, and legal characterization | The Guide is not a legal agreement or opinion |
| Information Security and Privacy | Govern access, credentials, certificates, testing, monitoring, incident response, data, and change | Production secrets and regulated data remain under approved institutional control |
| Finance / Accounting | Define accounting, financial reporting, provider-charge treatment, reconciliation, and records requirements | Platform state does not determine public accounting treatment |
| Internal Audit | Provides independent assurance according to its mandate | Does not become an implementation owner |
| External auditor / regulator / oversight body | Performs review, supervision, or assurance within applicable authority | Independent judgment is not replaced by platform evidence |
| ODTI | Licenses and maintains x-change, `emi-core`, supported components, documentation, testing capability, and agreed driver work | Does not provide public or banking authority, custody, settlement access, or production approval |
| 3neti / appointed engineering provider | Performs institution-specific discovery, mapping, driver implementation, testing, and maintenance where engaged | Does not own the institution's API authority, credentials, data, or production decision |
| DevOps / managed-service provider | Performs delegated provisioning, deployment, monitoring, backup, recovery, release support, and incident work | Does not own the environment, data, credentials, billing, or release authority |
| Settlement institution / payment provider | Performs supported financial movement and provides records under its agreement | Does not define the public program or complete banking ceremony |

## Decision Gates

1. **Program authorization** — The Board or delegated authority permits Management to evaluate and establish the Program.
2. **Authority and suitability** — Responsible public and banking functions determine whether the candidate workflow may proceed to design.
3. **Service definition** — The institutions approve the first public-finance ceremony and accountable owners.
4. **Architecture, data, and security approval** — Responsible functions approve the intended environment, information flows, and integration boundary.
5. **Driver certification** — Agreed technical behavior is evidenced under controlled conditions.
6. **Operational readiness** — The responsible institutions judge the complete ceremony ready for bounded production.
7. **Pilot decision** — The required authorities approve a limited production scope.
8. **Evidence review** — The institutions decide to Proceed, Correct, Extend, or Pause.

Actual legal, fiduciary, regulatory, procurement, banking, accounting, and audit responsibilities must be confirmed for the institution and workflow. This appendix does not allocate them by itself.
