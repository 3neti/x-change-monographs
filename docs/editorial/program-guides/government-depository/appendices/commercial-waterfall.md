# Appendix C — Commercial Waterfall

The Commercial Waterfall is a conceptual accountability model. It does not prescribe prices, revenue shares, public fees, procurement treatment, accounting treatment, taxes, or contractual terms.

## Economic Sequence

1. The responsible public and banking institutions define the authorized public-finance service and the depository institution's approved commercial treatment.
2. Service activity creates the applicable fee, income, cost, deposit, liquidity, relationship, or public value within the actual program and banking model.
3. Settlement-provider, network, cloud, identity, communications, and other third-party charges are recognized under applicable agreements.
4. Licensed software, implementation, institution-specific driver, maintenance, managed operations, infrastructure, support, security, reconciliation, and internal operating responsibilities are allocated to the responsible parties.
5. The depository institution evaluates residual financial and institutional value against its authority, strategy, risk appetite, service obligations, and approved financial case.
6. The responsible government entity evaluates price, service, evidence, accountability, and public value under its applicable authority and process.

## Cost and Responsibility Categories

| Category | What it covers | Governing instrument |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software license | Right to use and receive maintained x-change software within agreed scope | License agreement |
| Implementation | Institution and workflow discovery, configuration, integration, validation, and launch work | Statement of work or implementation agreement |
| Institution-specific driver | Discovery, mapping, implementation, certification, and agreed evolution of `3neti/emi-{institution}` | Driver scope and maintenance terms |
| Maintenance | Supported releases, compatibility, fixes, documentation, and agreed platform evolution | Maintenance terms |
| Managed operations | Delegated deployment and operating services | Managed-service agreement and institutional controls |
| Institution infrastructure | Cloud or approved hosting, domains, monitoring, security, backups, and related provider services | Institution-controlled provider agreements |
| Settlement and payment services | Account, participation, transaction, network, sponsor, foreign-exchange, and provider charges | Institution's settlement and provider agreements |
| Public-program administration | Authority, participant administration, communications, program operations, accounting, records, and public reporting | Applicable public authority, budget, process, and agreement |
| Internal institutional work | Governance, public-sector banking, Operations, Treasury, Technology, Risk, Compliance, Security, Finance, Audit, and support | Institution budgets and mandates |

Deposits and transaction activity are consequences of actual public-finance relationships, not guaranteed outputs of software licensing. No commercial flow changes custody, public authority, banking responsibility, settlement responsibility, or production control. Detailed amounts require separate evidence, negotiation, due diligence, and approval.
