# RBAP Digital Banking Program Guide

This directory contains the first sector implementation of the Canonical Banking Program Guide architecture.

- `rbap-digital-banking-program-guide.md` is the canonical guide manuscript. Its approved title hierarchy and section architecture are frozen. Sections 1–7 are editorially complete, locked, and byte-preserved as the canonical narrative. Appendices A–G are substantively complete within the manuscript, including the governed Mermaid diagram source in Appendix G. The Executive Summary remains **In Review** and is now eligible for revalidation under EDR-012; it has not yet been locked. Future narrative changes remain limited to factual corrections, typographical corrections, whole-manuscript cadence review, and cross-reference updates.
- `metadata.yaml` records its document identity, status, source path, and governance controls.
- [`../CANONICAL_BANKING_PROGRAM_GUIDE.md`](../CANONICAL_BANKING_PROGRAM_GUIDE.md) governs its structure.
- [`../PROGRAM_GUIDE_PARITY_CHECKLIST.md`](../PROGRAM_GUIDE_PARITY_CHECKLIST.md) records its coverage status.

The RBAP strategic white paper remains a separate thought-leadership document at [`../../rbap/00-modernizing-rural-banks.md`](../../rbap/00-modernizing-rural-banks.md).

Generated publication files belong under `build/`, not in this directory.

## Edition 1

Edition 1 publishes the first editorial freeze as a self-contained Board paper. Its scope remains the Executive Summary and Sections 1–7. The completed Appendices A–G and diagram sources have not yet been typeset or published, and the existing Edition 1 artifacts remain unchanged.
