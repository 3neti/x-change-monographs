# RBAP Digital Banking Program Guide

This directory contains the first sector implementation of the Canonical Banking Program Guide architecture.

- `rbap-digital-banking-program-guide.md` is the canonical and locked guide manuscript. Its approved title hierarchy and section architecture are frozen. The Executive Summary was revalidated under EDR-012 and locked without wording changes. Sections 1–7 remain editorially locked and byte-preserved, and Appendices A–G are locked as institutional reference source material, including the governed Mermaid source in Appendix G. Future source changes are limited to factual corrections, typographical corrections, and governed cross-reference updates.
- `metadata.yaml` records its document identity, status, source path, and governance controls.
- [`../CANONICAL_BANKING_PROGRAM_GUIDE.md`](../CANONICAL_BANKING_PROGRAM_GUIDE.md) governs its structure.
- [`../PROGRAM_GUIDE_PARITY_CHECKLIST.md`](../PROGRAM_GUIDE_PARITY_CHECKLIST.md) records its coverage status.

The RBAP strategic white paper remains a separate thought-leadership document at [`../../rbap/00-modernizing-rural-banks.md`](../../rbap/00-modernizing-rural-banks.md).

Generated publication files belong under `build/`, not in this directory.

## Edition 1

Edition 1 preserves the first editorial freeze as a self-contained Board paper. Its scope remains the Executive Summary and Sections 1–7, and its existing artifacts remain unchanged. The complete canonical source is now locked, but the appendices and diagram sources have not yet been typeset or published. Final publication governance, figure rendering, accessibility tagging, frozen-body substitution, and pagination remain pending.
