# Editorial Acceptance

## Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide

**Acceptance date:** 18 July 2026  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Edition:** First Approved Implementation Guide  
**Editorial lock:** Complete

## Accepted Scope

The accepted repository comprises:

- the Executive Summary;
- Sections 1–7;
- Appendices A–G;
- canonical thrift-bank Program Guide metadata;
- the completed parity record; and
- repository navigation identifying the Guide's place within the editorial system.

No publication edition, typeset artifact, legal agreement, pricing proposal, bank-specific proposal, or production authorization is included in this acceptance.

## Editorial Determination

The Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide is accepted as the canonical implementation guide for the thrift-bank track of the x-change ecosystem.

The Guide is the implementation reference from which future institution-specific thrift-bank editions should derive. Those editions may adapt institutional context, approved services, audience language, commercial emphasis, implementation sequence, and bank-specific arrangements. They must not establish competing architectural doctrine.

## Editorial Rationale

The Guide implements the EDR-005 architecture in full. It answers the seven canonical business questions, covers all mandatory Program Guide categories, completes Appendices A–G, preserves the institution-specific settlement model, and distinguishes the Program from the enduring Capability.

The bank remains the protagonist throughout. The Guide organizes banking authority, customer relationships, settlement capability, technology, and production governance without implying that x-change supplies or replaces them.

## Frozen Doctrine

The following propositions are frozen:

- The bank remains the protagonist.
- The bank brings the authority and the rails.
- x-change brings the operating grammar.
- The bank-specific driver connects them.
- Settlement connectivity is not a banking service.
- Banking capability exceeds payment capability.
- The banking service is the product.
- The Pay Code is the credential.
- Readiness is an institutional judgment.
- Driver certification is not operational readiness.
- One governed operating Capability supports many banking services.
- Remittance remains the canonical explanatory ceremony.

Future editions may improve institution-specific expression. They must not silently alter, dilute, or contradict these propositions. A proposed doctrinal change must return to the Institutional Study and the applicable editorial-governance process.

## Lock Consequences

- The canonical manuscript, appendices, and diagrams are editorially locked.
- Future canonical changes are limited to factual or typographical corrections, navigational maintenance, or changes authorized through explicit editorial governance.
- Institution-specific editions must identify this repository as their implementation source and the Institutional Study as their doctrinal source.
- Adaptation does not imply adoption, approval, certification, or participation by any named institution.

## Institution-Specific Editions

Future work proceeds through institution-specific editions. Such editions may adapt customer examples, banking ceremonies, implementation priorities, institutional terminology, deployment sequencing, governance emphasis, commercial proposals, and institution-specific implementation detail.

They exist to adapt the framework to an institution. They do not exist to redesign the framework. Editorial improvements discovered through field use must first be evaluated as potential doctrine. Canonical doctrine may change only through an explicit Editorial Decision Record.

## Final EDR-005 Decision

The Canonical Lock Ceremony is accepted. EDR-005 is editorially complete for the Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide.

> The canonical editorial work for the Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program is complete.

Future effort should shift from creating additional doctrine toward applying the canonical framework within specific financial institutions. Experience gained through actual institutional engagements should inform future editions and, where necessary, future Editorial Decision Records.

The repository now serves as the stable implementation reference for the thrift-bank track of the x-change ecosystem.
