# Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide

This directory contains the canonical thrift-bank implementation of the [Canonical Banking Program Guide](../CANONICAL_BANKING_PROGRAM_GUIDE.md) under EDR-005.

- [`thrift-bank-program-guide.md`](thrift-bank-program-guide.md) is the accepted and editorially locked canonical implementation guide.
- [`metadata.yaml`](metadata.yaml) records document identity, governance, source doctrine, and editorial status.
- [`appendices/`](appendices/) contains completed Appendices A–G.
- [`EDITORIAL_ACCEPTANCE.md`](EDITORIAL_ACCEPTANCE.md) records the permanent canonical lock decision and frozen doctrine.
- [`../PROGRAM_GUIDE_PARITY_CHECKLIST.md`](../PROGRAM_GUIDE_PARITY_CHECKLIST.md) records architectural and business-question parity.

The approved Phase 1 [Institutional Study](../../thrift-bank-digital-banking-program/README.md) remains the authoritative doctrinal source. The Guide operationalizes that doctrine; it does not replace or modify it.

Future institution-specific thrift-bank editions must derive from this repository. They may adapt approved institutional context and implementation detail without silently altering the frozen doctrine.

The canonical publishing program contains two editions. Both derive from the Institutional Study and the same frozen Program Guide doctrine. Neither supersedes the other. Future work proceeds through institution-specific editions unless an explicit Editorial Decision Record reopens canonical doctrine.

## Executive Edition

The **Executive Edition**, First Edition, Version 1.0, is the concise Board paper for Boards of Directors, presidents, chief executives, and executive committees. Its scope is the cover, publication information, table of contents, Executive Summary, and Sections 1–7. It intentionally excludes appendices.

## Institutional Reference Edition

The **Institutional Reference Edition**, First Edition, Version 1.0, is the comprehensive implementation reference for executive leadership and responsible Technology, Operations, Treasury, Risk, Compliance, implementation, and partner functions. Its source was developed under the working name Expanded Edition. It develops institutional reasoning, operating perspectives, governance, banking ceremonies, and the launch journey while preserving the doctrine of the Executive Edition.

## Institution-Specific Editions

Future AllBank, PSBank, City Savings Bank, Philippine Business Bank, CARD SME Bank, or other institution-specific editions adapt the canonical framework to an institution. They do not redefine it. They must remain doctrinally traceable to the Institutional Study and both canonical publication editions.

[AllBank Institution Intelligence](../../../institutions/allbank/README.md) is the first evidence-based application under EDR-015. It remains a research and mapping repository, not an Institution Edition or adoption record.
