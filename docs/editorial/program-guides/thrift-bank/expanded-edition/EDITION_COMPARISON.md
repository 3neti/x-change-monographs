# Thrift Bank Digital Banking Program Guide — Edition Comparison

## Purpose

The First Edition and Expanded Edition are separate, valid expressions of the same canonical thrift-bank doctrine. This note prevents the Expanded Edition from being mistaken for a correction or replacement of the published First Edition.

## Edition Roles

| Dimension | First Edition | Expanded Edition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary role | Concise Board paper | Comprehensive institutional implementation reference |
| Primary audience | Board and executive leadership | Board, executive leadership, and responsible implementation functions |
| Editorial state | Canonical, locked, and published | Editorial review draft |
| Narrative scope | Executive Summary and Sections 1–7 | Expanded Executive Summary, Sections 1–7, and shared Appendices A–G |
| Explanatory approach | Compressed institutional argument | Developed reasoning, operating perspectives, scenarios, and governance gates |
| Doctrine | Frozen | Inherited without modification |
| Publication artifact | First Edition PDF and HTML | None; publication is outside the present assignment |

## Relationship Between the Editions

The First Edition remains the appropriate document for concise Board circulation. It states the institutional proposition, boundaries, operating grammar, representative ceremonies, institutional case, and launch path without requiring the reader to study implementation detail.

The Expanded Edition serves a different reading need. It develops the reasoning beneath the same proposition so that executive sponsors and responsible functions can understand why the operating model is governed as described, how the components relate, what each function must observe, and how the institution moves from approval to controlled production.

The Expanded Edition does not supersede the First Edition. It does not reopen the canonical doctrine, alter the architecture, or change the role of the Institutional Study. Both editions remain doctrinally traceable to the same accepted sources.

## Preserved Doctrine

Both editions preserve these propositions:

- the bank remains the protagonist;
- the bank brings the authority and the rails;
- x-change brings the operating grammar;
- the bank-specific driver connects them;
- settlement connectivity is not a banking service;
- banking capability exceeds payment capability;
- the banking service is the product;
- the Pay Code is the credential;
- readiness is an institutional judgment;
- driver certification is not operational readiness; and
- one governed operating Capability supports many banking services.

## Change Rules

The locked First Edition may be changed only under its existing canonical governance. Expanded Edition review should improve clarity and institutional understanding without silently changing doctrine. Any proposed doctrinal change must be evaluated through a new Editorial Decision Record before either edition is revised.

Institution-specific editions inherit from the canonical guide. They may adapt approved institutional context and implementation detail, but they do not derive new doctrine from editorial elaboration in the Expanded Edition.
