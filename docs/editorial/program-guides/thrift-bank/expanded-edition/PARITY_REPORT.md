# Expanded Thrift Bank Program Guide — RBAP Parity Report

## Assessment

**Status:** Accepted and complete

The Institutional Reference Edition exhibits comparable institutional maturity to the RBAP Digital Banking Program Guide. The guides do not use identical headings or examples because they address different institutional contexts. Parity is established through the business questions answered, explanatory depth, governance treatment, operational walkthroughs, and implementation discipline.

The Editorial Board has accepted this assessment. The Institutional Reference Edition is locked and published under EDR-013.

## Coverage by Section

### Executive Summary

The Summary now stands alone as a Board briefing. It explains the institutional context, why connectivity is insufficient, the Program/Capability distinction, ownership boundaries, operating grammar, remittance, recurring relationships, launch journey, and Board decision.

### Section 1

The section develops the evolution of thrift-bank relationships, limits of channel accumulation, cost of fragmented operating models, customer expectations, recurring activity, and institutional continuity.

### Section 2

The section distinguishes Program from Capability and develops the responsibilities of the bank, ODTI, the bank-specific driver, managed operations, settlement providers, existing systems, governance, licensing, and commercial boundaries.

### Section 3

The section explains operating leverage, customer relevance, CASA and recurring activity, employer, merchant and SME relationships, housing, institutional optionality, technology investment, and long-term economics without projections.

### Section 4

The section teaches Funding Event, Treasury, Lien, Facility, Settlement Envelope, Pay Code, Execution, evidence, and reconciliation as connected institutional responsibilities. Custody, authority, driver operation, and readiness remain explicit.

### Section 5

The canonical remittance ceremony is observed from customer, Operations, Treasury, Technology, settlement, Management, and Support perspectives. Payroll, collections, merchant services, SME disbursement, housing, and customer-directed settlement demonstrate reuse without introducing new grammar.

### Section 6

The section develops relationships before transactions, Transaction Gravity, resilience, adaptability, institutional capability, commercial sustainability, common learning, and the Board's institutional decision.

### Section 7

The section presents the complete institutional journey from sponsorship and discovery through driver mapping, certification, complete-operation testing, readiness, controlled production, evidence review, expansion, and governed learning.

## Canonical Boundary Check

- No RBAP association-governance model was imported into the thrift-bank guide.
- No common settlement provider is required.
- No banking authority, custody, customer relationship, or production decision moves to x-change or ODTI.
- The Program and Capability remain distinct.
- The bank-specific driver remains institution-specific engineering requiring discovery, mapping, implementation, certification, and maintenance.
- Technical certification remains narrower than operational readiness.
- Shared canonical diagrams and appendices were not redesigned or doctrinally altered.

## Publication Determination

Editorial parity is accepted. Publication has been completed without reopening architecture or altering the canonical manuscript. Future work proceeds through institution-specific editions unless an explicit Editorial Decision Record reopens canonical doctrine.
