# Appendix C — Commercial Waterfall

The Commercial Waterfall is a conceptual accountability model. It does not prescribe prices, revenue shares, accounting treatment, taxes, or contractual terms.

## Economic Sequence

1. The bank defines the customer or institutional banking service and its approved commercial treatment.
2. Service activity creates the applicable income, fee, cost, or relationship value within the bank's product model.
3. Settlement-provider and third-party charges are recognized under the bank's agreements.
4. Licensed-software, implementation, bank-specific driver, maintenance, managed-operations, infrastructure, support, and internal operating responsibilities are allocated to the responsible parties.
5. The bank evaluates the residual financial and institutional value against its strategy, risk appetite, and approved financial case.

## Cost and Responsibility Categories

| Category | What it covers | Governing instrument |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Software license | Right to use and receive maintained x-change software within agreed scope | License agreement |
| Implementation | Institution discovery, configuration, integration, validation, and launch work | Statement of work or implementation agreement |
| Bank-specific driver | Discovery, mapping, implementation, certification, and agreed evolution of `3neti/emi-{bank}` | Driver scope and maintenance terms |
| Maintenance | Supported releases, compatibility, fixes, documentation, and agreed platform evolution | Maintenance terms |
| Managed operations | Delegated deployment and operating services | Managed-service agreement and bank controls |
| Bank infrastructure | Cloud or approved hosting, domains, monitoring, security, backups, and related provider services | Bank-controlled provider agreements |
| Settlement and payment services | Account, participation, transaction, network, and provider charges | Bank's settlement and provider agreements |
| Internal institutional work | Governance, product, operations, Treasury, Technology, Risk, Compliance, Security, Finance, Audit, and support | Bank budgets and mandates |

No commercial flow changes custody, banking authority, customer ownership, settlement responsibility, or production control. Detailed amounts require separate due diligence and approval.
