# Reader Participant Information and Consent

## Purpose

You are invited to take part in a short reader study about visual explanations used in x-change marketing publications.

> **We are testing the visual design—not you.**

There are no right words to use. We want to understand what the visuals communicate when someone sees them for the first time.

## What you will do

You may be asked to:

- look at several visual explanations for a limited time;
- describe what you think is happening;
- answer short questions about what moved, who acted, and what you remember;
- view a version without labels; and
- sketch or describe the sequence from memory.

The session is not a test of financial, technical, or design knowledge.

## Voluntary participation

Taking part is voluntary. You may skip a question or stop the session at any time without giving a reason. Choosing not to participate will have no penalty or effect on your relationship with the study team or its organizations.

## Recording policy

The facilitator will explain which records are planned before the session. Nothing beyond the choices confirmed below will be recorded.

Possible records include written notes, audio, video, screen activity, and sketches. You may consent to some forms of recording and decline others.

## Use of responses and quotations

Responses will be used to evaluate and improve the visual design. Findings are normally reported in de-identified or aggregate form.

The study team will not use an attributed quotation unless you explicitly permit attribution. Anonymous quotations may be used only when consented to below and when reasonable steps have been taken to remove identifying context.

## Withdrawal
