# VIS-001 Reader-Testing Protocol

> **Historical protocol:** This Milestone 5 document is preserved as the prototype-stage precursor. Formal Milestone 6 studies follow the canonical [Reader Validation Workflow](../../READER_VALIDATION_WORKFLOW.md) and its reusable evidence templates.

## Purpose

Test whether people unfamiliar with x-change can understand and later remember the fixed VIS-001 meaning across different visual directions.

This protocol prepares future research. It contains no test results.

## Participants

Recruit first-time viewers who have not read the x-change Editorial corpus or worked on the Marketing Design System.

Where possible, include a balanced mix of:

- banking executives;
- operations, treasury, or payments practitioners;
- public-sector or institutional leaders;
- business users; and
- people without specialist financial-technology knowledge.

Record participant profile categories without collecting unnecessary personal information.

## Materials

- The eight prototype SVGs presented at consistent physical or screen size.
- A randomized presentation order for each participant.
- A blank sheet or digital canvas for the Memory Test.
- A neutral response form.
- A timing method.
- A record of surface conditions: viewing distance, device or print size, and ambient context.

Do not show direction names such as “Transit Map” or “Minimal Institutional” before responses. The names may prime interpretation.

## Session structure

### Part 1 — Unprompted Fifteen-Second Test

For each assigned prototype:

1. Show the image for fifteen seconds.
2. Remove it.
3. Ask: **What do you think is happening?**
4. Record the response verbatim.

Do not explain Pay Code, correct terminology, or ask leading follow-ups before the first answer is complete.

### Part 2 — Semantic questions

Ask in this order:

1. What is the Pay Code?
2. What moves?
3. Does money move with the Pay Code?
4. When does the recipient choose?
5. How many destinations are selected?
6. Who appears to complete the movement of money?
7. Where does the money go?
8. What are the three main steps?

Use the participant's language. Do not require canonical terms when the meaning is correct.

### Part 3 — Caption Test

Present a label-free variant or conceal the words where practical. Ask the participant to identify:

- the sender or origin;
- the credential;
- the recipient;
- the order of events;
- the choice moment; and
- the completion.

Record which meanings depended upon labels.

### Part 4 — Memory Test

After a neutral delay:

1. Ask the participant to sketch or describe the journey from memory.
2. Do not provide the words Generate, Share, or Claim.
3. Ask where Choice belongs.
4. Record the sketch or description without correction.

The canonical reference is:

```text
Generate → Share → Claim
                     └ choice
```

### Part 5 — Comparative preference

After semantic testing is complete, show the tested directions together and ask:

- Which direction is easiest to understand?
- Which is easiest to remember?
- Which feels most credible for a financial institution?
- Which would you notice at a conference?
- Which would you prefer to see explained in motion?
- What can be removed from your preferred direction?

Preference is recorded after comprehension so aesthetic reaction does not replace semantic evidence.

## Observation checklist

For each prototype record:

- first visual fixation described or observed;
- whether the participant identifies an intended payment before the Pay Code;
- whether the Pay Code is understood as credential, money, balance, ticket, or another object;
- what the participant believes moves during Share;
- whether payment is believed complete at Share;
- whether the recipient visibly acts;
- whether Choice is placed inside Claim;
- whether exactly one destination is selected;
- who appears to perform financial completion;
- whether universal acceptance is inferred;
- remembered number and order of steps; and
- any language indicating x-change custody or banking authority.

## Result coding

Use the following evidence codes:

| Code | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `C` | Correct without prompting |
| `P` | Partially correct or materially incomplete |
| `M` | Material misunderstanding |
| `N` | Not noticed or not recalled |

Do not convert qualitative observations into a numerical score until the sample and coding method have been approved.

## Pass criteria

A direction is a strong advancement candidate when unfamiliar readers consistently:

- identify Generate, Share, and Claim in order;
- understand that an intended payment precedes the Pay Code;
- describe the Pay Code as a credential, code, instruction, or equivalent rather than money;
- identify the credential as what moves during Share;
- place recipient choice inside Claim;
- see one selected destination and one completion;
- retain a participating institutional role in financial completion; and
- reproduce the three-step journey after a delay.

Any recurring stored-value, universal-acceptance, duplicated-payout, premature-completion, or x-change-custody interpretation is a Doctrine failure regardless of aesthetic preference.

## Order and bias controls

- Randomize prototype order.
- Do not reveal visual-family names before testing.
- Rotate which directions receive the full Caption Test when session length is limited.
- Keep size and exposure time consistent.
- Avoid praising, defending, or explaining a direction.
- Separate participant observations from facilitator interpretations.
- Preserve contradictory evidence rather than forcing consensus.

## Reporting

The later report should distinguish:

- observed participant responses;
- researcher interpretation;
- proposed visual correction; and
- advancement recommendation.

Do not state that a direction “passes” solely because participants prefer its appearance.

## Stop conditions

Pause testing and review the prototype if it repeatedly implies:

- money inside the Pay Code;
- payment completion during Share;
- choice before Claim;
- more than one completed destination;
- universal participation; or
- x-change as the financial institution.

Correct the failing prototype. Do not change VIS-001 to accommodate the misunderstanding.
