# VS0-D01 — Transit Journey

- **Status:** Non-production study
- **Asset:** `assets/marketing/prototypes/visual-sprint-zero/direction-01-transit.svg`
- **Primary influence:** D03 Transit Map
- **Question:** Does one legible credential moving through three stations improve sequence memory without becoming a money rail?

The same framed `7K9M-4R2P` object occupies Generate, Share, and Claim. Small SMS, chat, print, and optional QR cues sit beside Share rather than replacing the route object. Claim stops movement, reveals three bounded destinations, and continues through one selected path.

Validate route meaning, credential continuity, QR subordination, delayed recall, and one-destination clarity. No reader result is claimed.
