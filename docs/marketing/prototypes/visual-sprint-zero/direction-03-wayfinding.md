- **Status:** Non-production study
- **Asset:** `assets/marketing/prototypes/visual-sprint-zero/direction-03-wayfinding.svg`
- **Primary influence:** D05 Pictographic Wayfinding
- **Question:** Can a framed alphanumeric credential remain readable at event distance without behaving like a universal ticket?

Three high-contrast fields preserve the journey. The same `7K9M-4R2P` credential is the white-on-dark moving object. Share includes channel pictograms, with a small QR cue at equal or lower weight than SMS and print. Claim enlarges the human and bounded choice.

Validate distance comprehension, ticket interpretation, QR hierarchy, and institutional completion. No reader result is claimed.
