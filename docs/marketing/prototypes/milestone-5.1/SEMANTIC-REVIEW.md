## Remaining test risks

### D03

The transit route may still be interpreted as a settlement rail. Reader questioning must distinguish what occupies the line from what the line itself represents.

### D04

Fine editorial linework may lose the payment-purpose and provider distinctions at compact sizes.

### D05

The high-contrast credential may resemble a universally accepted ticket. Participating-option cues must be tested without relying on labels.

### D06

Modular containment may resemble product UI or an implementation workflow. The human recipient must remain more memorable than the cards.

### D01

The control is semantically explicit but structurally plain. It may establish a useful comprehension baseline without offering a desirable visual direction.

## Doctrine check

No corrected study intentionally implies:

- stored value or wallet balance;
- money creation;
- x-change as a bank, authority, or custodian;
- universal interoperability;
- payment completion during Share;
- recipient choice before Claim;
- simultaneous payout;
- duplicated redemption; or
- more than one completed destination.

Any such recurring reader interpretation is a Doctrine failure and requires correcting the prototype rather than changing VIS-001.

## Decision

D03, D04, D05, and D06 are ready for fair unfamiliar-reader testing. D01 is available as a control. D02 and D08 remain influences only. D07 remains on hold.

No production identity has been selected.
