# Milestone 5.1 Scale Review

## Purpose

Document how each shortlisted corrected composition behaves when the same hierarchy is mechanically rendered at three consistent sizes.

## Standard preview sizes

| Profile | Dimensions | Intended test |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Small Card | 480 × 260 px | Compact digital and social comprehension |
| Page | 1200 × 650 px | Normal reading and publication comprehension |
| Banner Distance | 1800 × 975 px | Large-format viewing from an increased distance |

All three preserve the same aspect ratio and composition. No profile was independently redesigned. The Banner Distance export tests enlargement and distance; it is not a final banner layout.

## D03 — Transit Map

- **Small Card:** Route and three credential states survive. Small participating labels do not; the visual must work without them.
- **Page:** Strongest overall reading. Payment intent, recipient, branch, and provider remain separable.
- **Banner Distance:** Route and selected branch dominate appropriately. The provider-completion caption becomes secondary.
- **Break risk:** The route may become more visually authoritative as scale increases, intensifying rail interpretation.

## D04 — Editorial Line

- **Small Card:** Human figures and credential states survive, but fine document and provider details weaken.
- **Page:** Human narrative, motion trace, and selected destination are balanced.
- **Banner Distance:** Major figures and black arrived credential remain visible; fine-line editorial character may recede.
- **Break risk:** The direction loses nuance before it loses basic sequence.

## D05 — Pictographic Wayfinding

- **Small Card:** Highest contrast and strongest silhouette preservation. Small participating labels disappear without harming the branch structure.
- **Page:** Credential path and enlarged Claim are immediately visible.
- **Banner Distance:** Strongest expected distance candidate. Black fields and white credential path remain legible.
- **Break risk:** Extreme clarity may strengthen the mistaken impression of a universally accepted ticket or network.

## D06 — Modular Card

- **Small Card:** Three modules and larger Claim remain identifiable; interior payment-purpose detail becomes quiet.
- **Page:** Strong semantic containment and continuity.
- **Banner Distance:** The larger Claim field reads well, but card boundaries may compete with the journey.
- **Break risk:** At some sizes the composition may be remembered as numbered workflow cards rather than human action.

## Cross-scale findings

- D05 preserves the most information at distance.
- D06 adapts most predictably across the three sizes.
- D03 preserves order most strongly.
- D04 preserves human meaning best at page scale.
- Small semantic labels cannot be relied upon in any direction.
- Credential continuity and singular destination must remain understandable from shape and relationship alone.

These findings are visual-inspection observations, not reader-test results.
