# VIS-001 Prototype Correction Brief

## Objective

Prepare the strongest Milestone 5 directions for fair reader testing by correcting visual continuity rather than improving style.

## Canonical visual invariant

Every corrected direction must show:

```text
the Pay Code appears
        ↓
the same Pay Code moves
        ↓
the same Pay Code reaches the recipient
        ↓
movement stops at Claim
        ↓
one supported destination is selected
```

## Continuity method

The corrected static studies use a shared continuity convention adapted to each visual family:

- one unmistakable Pay Code silhouette;
- earlier positions shown as outline or motion states;
- one continuous, subordinate credential trace;
- the final Pay Code rendered as the arrived state beside the recipient;
- no visual motif resembling coins, balances, or funds; and
- no path continuing into provider completion as though the credential itself settles money.

The provider-completion relationship begins only after the recipient selects one destination. It is visually distinct from the credential trace.

## Generate correction

Generate begins with a recognizable payment-purpose cue inside a quiet governed boundary. Authorization appears as a small check or gate between purpose and credential. The institution remains supporting context rather than the visual subject.

## Share correction

The Pay Code itself occupies the communication path. People, channel cues, or route structures remain contextual. A viewer should not be able to explain Share as two people chatting while ignoring the credential.

## Claim correction

Claim receives the strongest human emphasis. The recipient visibly presents the arrived credential. A bounded choice opens locally. One supported destination is selected. The provider-completion trace begins after selection and remains secondary.

## Corrected directions

- D01 — neutral control
- D03 — transit-map candidate
- D04 — editorial-line candidate
- D05 — wayfinding candidate
- D06 — modular-card candidate and structural baseline

## Influence and hold directions

D02 contributes warmth, approachable proportion, and interpersonal feeling. D08 contributes restraint, whitespace, and executive credibility. Neither is prepared as a full reader-test candidate in this pass. D07 remains on hold because dimensional complexity has not demonstrated a comprehension advantage.

## Test assets

Each shortlisted direction includes:

- corrected labeled SVG;
- intentional label-free SVG;
- small-card PNG preview;
- page PNG preview; and
- banner-distance PNG preview.

D01 is retained as a corrected labeled control and does not receive the full test-export set.

## Non-production boundary

These assets remain prototypes. They do not establish final color, typography, brand style, publication layout, or production illustration masters.
