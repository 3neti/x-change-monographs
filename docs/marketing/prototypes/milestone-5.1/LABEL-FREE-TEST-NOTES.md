# Milestone 5.1 Label-Free Test Notes

## Purpose

The dedicated label-free SVGs support the Caption Test without hiding text at presentation time or leaving composition gaps created for labels.

## Removed from each variant

- visual-family name;
- prototype identifier;
- Generate, Share, and Claim headings;
- explanatory footer;
- payment-purpose labels;
- Choice and selected-destination labels;
- provider-completion labels; and
- all other visible text.

Accessible `<title>` and `<desc>` elements remain in the SVG source. They are not visually rendered and preserve nonvisual identification of the test asset.

## Retained visual semantics

- recognizable payment-purpose document and quiet approval;
- one stable credential silhouette;
- outline motion states connected by one subordinate trace;
- one solid arrived credential at Claim;
- visible recipient action;
- bounded local choice;
- one selected destination; and
- provider completion on a distinct secondary relationship.

## Direction-specific questions

### D01 Flat Geometric control

Does the neutral geometry communicate one credential journey without relying on labels, or does the credential become more important than the people and intended outcome?

### D03 Transit Map

Does the viewer follow the credential marker, or describe the line itself as the payment rail?

### D04 Editorial Line

Does the viewer identify the intended payment and credential transfer without editorial captions?

### D05 Wayfinding

Does the viewer interpret the repeated double-bar card as one credential rather than a ticket accepted everywhere?

### D06 Modular Card

Does the viewer see one journey across modules rather than three independent instructions or software screens?

## Use rule

Show the label-free asset before the corrected labeled asset. Record the unprompted explanation. Do not reveal direction names or canonical terms until the Caption Test response is complete. Include D01 only when its role and presentation conditions were fixed in the study manifest before evidence collection.

No Caption Test results are claimed in this milestone.
