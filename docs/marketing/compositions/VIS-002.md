# VIS-002 — Institutional Network

## Record

- **Class:** Supporting composition
- **Status:** Canonical concept
- **Question answered:** How can institutions participate without becoming one institution?
- **Core insight:** Distinct institutions retain their roles while a common operating layer coordinates the governed instruction.

## Reading order

1. The responsible institution generates the Pay Code for an authorized instruction.
2. x-change supports operating coordination without replacing institutional roles.
3. A participating provider performs its part of the supported Claim journey.

## Primary hierarchy

1. Separate institutional boundaries.
2. Governed instruction and completion.
3. Quiet connective operating layer.

## Nested complexity

Settlement and evidence may appear when needed, but neither becomes the public story. Infrastructure remains behind the people and institutional responsibilities it supports.

## Permitted adaptations

- Side-by-side or stacked institutions.
- Omission of evidence when accountability is not the question.
- Replacement of generic institution types with approved audience-neutral labels.

## Prohibited mutations

- A shared bank or pooled institution.
- x-change as custodian, regulator, settlement bank, or financial provider.
- A universal switch or provider network.
- Institutional architecture preceding VIS-001 in first-introduction collateral.

## Accessibility summary

A responsible institution and a participating financial provider remain distinct and retain their respective authority and responsibility while x-change supports coordination of the governed instruction between them.

Required: Caption Test and Doctrine Test. Use the Fifteen-Second Test when VIS-002 appears in executive or event collateral.
