# VIS-004 — Public Purpose

## Record

- **Class:** Supporting composition
- **Status:** Canonical concept
- **Question answered:** Can the same visual grammar support accountable public assistance?
- **Core insight:** Public authority, human dignity, conditions, completion, and evidence remain visible within the same Pay Code journey.

## Composition

```text
ILL-008 Government Benefits     ILL-012 Emergency Assistance

public purpose → Pay Code → intended person → completion + evidence
```

## Source illustrations

- ILL-008 — Government Benefits
- ILL-012 — Emergency Assistance

## Semantic icon dependencies

ICN-001 Person, ICN-002 Institution, ICN-003 Pay Code, ICN-004 Instruction, ICN-007 Approval, ICN-008 Validation, ICN-012 Evidence, and ICN-015 Completion.

## Reading order

1. A responsible public institution establishes the authorized purpose.
2. The Pay Code reaches the intended person.
3. Applicable conditions remain part of Claim.
4. Completion produces appropriate evidence.

## Primary hierarchy

1. Intended public outcome and recipient dignity.
2. Responsible authority.
3. Quiet conditions and evidence.

## Nested complexity

Urgency may distinguish emergency assistance, but urgency does not remove governance. Evidence is more visible than in general commercial examples but remains secondary to the human outcome.

## Permitted adaptations

- Display the two scenarios together or use one in a governed public-purpose publication.
- Adjust the prominence of evidence to the audience question.
- Use neutral public-institution cues without political identity.

## Prohibited mutations

- Political branding or implied endorsement.
- Entitlement or eligibility guarantees.
- Disaster spectacle or diminished recipient dignity.
- x-change as public authority.
- Bypassed conditions or controls.

## Accessibility summary

A responsible public institution authorizes a benefit or emergency-assistance instruction. The intended person presents the Pay Code through a supported journey, applicable conditions remain in force, and completion creates appropriate evidence.

## Validation profile

Required: Caption Test and Doctrine Test. Include a dignity review whenever people experiencing hardship are represented.
