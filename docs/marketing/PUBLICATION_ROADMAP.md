# Marketing Publication Roadmap

## Objective

Develop one canonical marketing knowledge base that can produce concise, consistent collateral across print, web, presentation, event, and social formats without redefining Pay Code for each medium.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Scope | Status | Exit condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Architecture Baseline | Family architecture, messaging grammar, visual grammar, illustration taxonomy, style guide, universal flyer, brochure, landing page, and conference sheet | Complete | Canonical Markdown and YAML exist; terminology and links validate |
| 1.1 — Marketing Compression | Generate → Share → Claim public sequence, compressed collateral, nested recipient choice, added Loan Proceeds and Incentives, normalized metadata | Complete | A first-time reader can understand the core idea from the headline and visual sequence |
| 2 — Illustration System and Visual Storyboarding | Semantic studies for ILL-001 through ILL-006, VIS-001 and application storyboards, reusable wireframes, and composition hierarchy | Complete | Canonical visual language is specified in Markdown before artwork or rendering |

| 7 — First Renderers | Reusable composition renderer and first publication-prototype pipeline | Planned | One command produces review-proof assets without rewriting source or composition semantics |
| 8 — Review and Message Lock | Publication-level review and approved copy and composition lock | Planned | Primary message understood within one minute; VIS-001 passes the Memory Test; boundaries remain accurate |
| 9 — Extended Collateral | Social cards, presentation slides, website modules, animation studies, and trade-show display variants | Planned | New formats reuse governed messages, semantic icons, illustration IDs, and canonical compositions |
| 10 — Institution Adaptation | Controlled sector and institution variants derived from the universal series | Planned | Adaptations remain traceable and introduce no new doctrine |
| 11 — Publication Freeze | Approved universal marketing series and governed asset library | Planned | Versioned sources, renderers, manifests, checksums, and freeze record exist |

## Priority sequence

1. Complete the operational launch gate and precommit RV-001.
2. Test D03–D06 with unfamiliar readers under the fixed study protocol.
3. Preserve raw observations outside public source control and commit only approved anonymized evidence.
4. Narrow or combine characteristics only after comprehension evidence exists.
5. Begin production language and renderers only after evidence-backed convergence.

## Research and review questions

- Can a first-time reader repeat the primary message after 30 seconds?
- Does the reader understand that the Pay Code does not hold money?
- Does recipient choice feel useful without appearing universal or ungoverned?
- Can the core illustration sequence work without UI screenshots?
- Does every format lead naturally to a deeper governed publication or conversation?

## Stop conditions

Do not refine D03–D06, create a new visual family, or begin production artwork, palette, typography, collateral, or renderer optimization before reader evidence supports convergence. Do not create institution-specific marketing collateral before the universal series has an evidence-backed production language. Do not use preference or engagement metrics to justify claims that contradict canonical doctrine.
