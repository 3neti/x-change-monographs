# Correspondence

Correspondence is the governed publication family for formal institutional communications that accompany, transmit, explain, acknowledge, or follow up on approved publications and submission portfolios.

Correspondence records what was communicated and how it was delivered. It does not own, revise, or duplicate the publications to which it refers.

## Record integrity

Every correspondence record must distinguish among exact sent correspondence, reconstructed sent correspondence, drafts, and reusable templates. A sent record is historical evidence and must not be silently rewritten.
